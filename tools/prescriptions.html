Ready prescriptions — dose, duration, counselling, reference wording

Reference wording for common primary-care regimens, by condition. Each card opens to what the cited guidance and the product information (SPC) state — indication · contraindications · drug table (dose · freq · route · duration) · alternative regimens · counselling points · follow-up · red flags · example record wording · source links — with links to the full protocol where one exists.

💊 Doses are written in our own words from NICE guidance and the manufacturers’ product information (SPC), for education. Always check the current SPC, BNF/BNFC, local formulary and the patient before prescribing. A few rows give a children’s dose (for example mg/kg); those rows say “Children: check the BNFC” and link to it. Links: SPC (emc) · BNF · BNFC (children).

Educational use only. Every prescription follows the NICE / FSRH or other guidance cited on the card, with doses checked against the product SPC. Always verify dose, contraindications, drug interactions, and your local formulary before prescribing — and confirm patient-specific factors (renal function, pregnancy, allergies, concomitant medications). Not a substitute for clinical judgement.
