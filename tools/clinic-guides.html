Clinic Guides

2-page printable desk guides for the conditions you see every day.

Page 1 takes you from the first raised reading, result or symptom to a confirmed diagnosis and the first treatment. Page 2 is the review: targets, the next step up, monitoring and when to refer. Print them, keep them on the desk, and open the full protocol when you need the detail and the evidence.

  • CheckedEvery figure checked against current NICE/MHRA guidance (October 2026).
  • National guidanceBuilt on NICE, MHRA and the UK national and society guidance they point to. Each guide names its sources.
  • Review datesEach guide shows when it was reviewed and when its next review is due.
  • Two A4 pagesPrints on two A4 sheets exactly. Each guide links to its full protocol or pathway.
Access: Clinic Guides are part of the Silver (Clinic) and Platinum plans. Guides marked Free are open to everyone, no sign-up needed. See the plans
