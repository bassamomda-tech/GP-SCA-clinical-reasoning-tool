Low mood — new presentation in adults

For: adults presenting with low mood in primary care. Not for: under 18s (CAMHS, NICE NG134). Pregnant or within a year of birth: use the perinatal routes in blocks 0–2.
Reviewed October 2026 · next review April 2027
NICE NG222NG225CG192CG185
Core rule: ask about suicidal thoughts directly and screen for past mania before any antidepressant. Use the conversation, not a score or a low/medium/high label, to plan safety.

0Stop — call 999 now — before any history

  • Overdose taken, serious self-injury or attempt in progress → 999 — do not wait for symptoms after an overdose
  • Immediate danger to life from suicidal intent → 999
  • Postpartum psychosis with risk to self or baby → 999 or crisis team

2Screen — one row is enough

  • Suicidal intent, a plan, or unable to stay safe → crisis team Same-day (NHS 111 option 2 out of hours)
  • Psychotic symptoms or severe self-neglect, not eating or drinking → Same-day
  • Possible mania: little need for sleep, pressured speech, grandiosity, risk-taking → Same-day if acute or at risk
  • Perinatal warning signs: rapid change, violent self-harm thoughts, estrangement from the baby → perinatal team Same-day
  • Safeguarding: children or dependants at risk, domestic abuse → Same-day local procedures
  • Older adult with acute confusion → delirium Same-day

3Assess — targeted, not blanket

☐ Mood, anhedonia, associated symptoms, function  ☐ Thoughts, intent, plan, means, previous self-harm, protective factors  ☐ Mania screen, family history  ☐ AUDIT-C, drugs, medicines review  ☐ Mental state, weight, pulse, BP

Bloods by indication: TSH, FBC + ferritin, B12 + folate, HbA1c, U&E, LFTs, calcium. Sodium if older; ECG before citalopram or escitalopram with cardiac risk.

PHQ-9 measures severity — item 9 is not a risk assessment.

4Classify — only if the screen is negative

  • Less severe depression — guided self-help or group CBT/behavioural activation via Talking Therapies.
  • More severe depression — offer individual CBT plus an antidepressant.
  • Grief or adjustment — support, bereavement services, review in 2–4 weeks.
  • Alcohol or drug-related — brief intervention; mood often lifts within weeks of stopping.
  • Organic or drug-induced — hypothyroidism, anaemia, B12, OSA, culprit medicines → treat the cause.
  • Uncertain — say so, keep the safety plan, review in 2 weeks.

5Treat today — doses per SPC, BNF and local formulary

  • Less severe: do not routinely offer an antidepressant first-line unless the person prefers it.
  • If starting: an SSRI — sertraline usual adult dose 50 mg once daily. Warn: 2–4 weeks to work, early agitation, possible early suicidal thoughts (same-day contact), do not stop suddenly.
  • Overdose risk: limited quantities (1–2 weeks); avoid tricyclics.
  • Today: written safety plan, crisis numbers in the phone, Talking Therapies self-referral, fit note if needed.

6Refer routinely

  • Suspected bipolar without acute risk → specialist assessment Urgent.
  • Moderate or severe perinatal illness → perinatal mental health (CG192).
  • Older adult with cognitive impairment → memory service. Under 18 → CAMHS.
  • Alcohol or drug services; bereavement support; social prescribing; debt advice.

7Never reassure by

  • A low score on a risk tool or "no plan".
  • A PHQ-9 item 9 of zero.
  • Starting an SSRI without asking about past highs.
  • "It is just grief" when symptoms keep deepening.
  • Calling it depression in an older adult before considering delirium and dementia.
  • Assuming low mood in pregnancy or after birth is the baby blues after 2 weeks.

8Safety-net — say it, then document it

999 “Call 999 if you have taken an overdose, injured yourself seriously, or are in immediate danger.” Same-day “If the thoughts get stronger, you make a plan or you feel you can’t keep yourself safe, call the crisis line or NHS 111 option 2 today. Tell us the same day if you feel agitated after starting the tablet, or high with little need for sleep.”

Follow-up: 1 week after starting an antidepressant if aged 18–25 or at increased risk; otherwise 2 weeks; no drug started → 2–4 weeks with repeat PHQ-9.
