Acne vulgaris

For: acne vulgaris managed in primary care. Not for: look-alikes (rosacea, perioral dermatitis, folliculitis, drug-induced acne, hidradenitis suppurativa) or rapidly virilising acne (endocrine pathway). Isotretinoin is started only by a consultant dermatologist-led team.
Reviewed October 2026 · next review April 2027
NICE NG198 (updated Apr 2026)MHRA isotretinoin (Jan 2026)MHRA co-cyprindiolGM antimicrobial guidanceBNF
Core rule: count the lesions, commit to one combination for 12 weeks, never use an antibiotic alone or a topical with an oral antibiotic, and refer before the scars set.

0Act now — check first, every time

  • Acne fulminans (sudden severe ulcerated acne, often fever, joint pain, unwell) → Same-day on-call dermatology, assessed within 24 hours. No isotretinoin from primary care.
  • Current suicidal ideation or a plan → Same-day local crisis pathway
  • On isotretinoin: new depression, marked mood change or self-harm → Urgent prescribing team; suicidal ideation or imminent risk → Same-day emergency mental health
  • On isotretinoin or a tetracycline: headache with visual disturbance (intracranial hypertension) → Same-day assessment incl. fundi; stop the tetracycline or isotretinoin, tell the prescriber.
  • Possible pregnancy on isotretinoin → stop it, pregnancy test → Same-day prescribing team

1Confirm — count, don't guess (any one is enough)

NG198 bandLesion count
Mild to moderateAny comedones · up to 34 inflammatory lesions · up to 2 nodules
Moderate to severe≥35 inflammatory lesions · ≥3 nodules

The band is not isotretinoin eligibility. No comedones + flushing → think rosacea.

2Check — every new presentation

☐ Lesion count and sites (face, back, chest)  ☐ Scarring and pigmentation  ☐ Mood, self-harm, picking  ☐ Pregnancy plans and contraception  ☐ Drug triggers (steroids, lithium, progestogen-only)

Don't miss: new acne with rapid hirsutism, deepening voice or clitoromegaly → investigate an androgen source before treating it as acne.

3Decide — choose with the person

  • One first-line option for 12 weeks, by severity, preference, pregnancy plans, irritation and cost. Do not stack them.
  • Mild acne → non-antibiotic topical and self-care; no oral antibiotics (GM guidance).
  • Skin care: pH-neutral cleanser twice daily, no picking, non-comedogenic products; no special diet.

4Target — judge the course at 12 weeks

At 12 weeksAction
ClearStop oral antibiotic, keep topical; consider maintenance
Improved, not clearContinue the oral antibiotic up to a further 12 weeks
Not improvedCheck adherence, irritation → switch option
Antibiotic >6 monthsExceptional only; review every 3 months

5Treat — NG198 first-line options, 12 weeks each

Any severityAdapalene + benzoyl peroxide gel
or tretinoin + clindamycin gel
Mild to moderate onlyBenzoyl peroxide + clindamycin gel
Moderate to severeAdapalene + benzoyl peroxide plus lymecycline or doxycycline
Moderate to severeAzelaic acid 20% plus lymecycline or doxycycline
  • Oral: lymecycline 408 mg or doxycycline 100 mg once daily; unsuitable → trimethoprim or a macrolide. Minocycline: not normally (GM).
  • Pregnancy or planning: avoid topical retinoids and oral tetracyclines (also tetracyclines in breastfeeding, under 12s) → benzoyl peroxide or azelaic acid.
  • Hormonal options (women) are second line. Doses per BNF and local formulary.

6Monitor

  • Antibiotic: write the stop date in the notes.
  • Maintenance (frequent relapse): adapalene + benzoyl peroxide; review after 12 weeks. Never an antibiotic.
  • Relapse: another 12-week first-line course; moderate to severe after isotretinoin → or re-refer.
  • Isotretinoin: record as a specialist medicine so tetracyclines and vitamin A are caught.

7Refer — consider: consultant dermatologist-led team

  • Mild to moderate, no response to 2 completed courses; moderate to severe, no response to a course with an oral antibiotic.
  • Scarring, persistent pigmentation, conglobate or nodulocystic acne, diagnostic uncertainty, persistent distress. Keep a topical going.
  • Mental health services (consider): significant distress, past suicidal ideation or self-harm, severe depression or anxiety, body dysmorphic disorder.

8Never

  • Antibiotic monotherapy (topical or oral), or a topical with an oral antibiotic.
  • A tetracycline with isotretinoin (intracranial hypertension); vitamin A with isotretinoin.
  • Isotretinoin started in primary care — refer; contraception 4 weeks before to 1 month after (PPP).
  • Co-cyprindiol with another hormonal contraceptive, or with current or previous meningioma.

9Safety-net — say it, then document it

Same day “If your spots suddenly become severe, open or crusted, with fever, joint pain or feeling unwell — contact us the same day. Thoughts of harming yourself — get help today.”   Treatment: “These creams work slowly — most people see a difference at around two months and the full effect at three. If it stings, use it every other night rather than giving up.”   Isotretinoin: “Low mood or mood change — contact us or the skin team urgently. A bad headache with blurred vision, or a possible pregnancy — the same day.”
