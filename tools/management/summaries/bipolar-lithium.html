Bipolar disorder and lithium

For: adults with suspected or diagnosed bipolar disorder, and lithium shared care in primary care. Not for: children and young people (CAMHS; BNFC), acute inpatient treatment, or unipolar depression (depression protocol).
Reviewed October 2026 · next review April 2027
NICE CG185 (updated 2025)NICE QS95NICE CG192MHRA valproateNPSA lithium
Core rule: mania or severe depression with risk is a same-day referral; never start an antidepressant without an antimanic drug; and lithium is only as safe as its monitoring and the interactions you avoid.

0Act now

  • Suspected mania or severe depression with danger to self or others → crisis or home treatment team Same-day; imminent risk to life → 999
  • Lithium toxicity — coarse tremor, vomiting, diarrhoea, ataxia, slurred speech, confusion, seizures → withhold lithium, hospital assessment and urgent level Same-day
  • Level above 1.5 mmol/L or symptoms → Same-day hospital. Above 1.0 mmol/L → check timing, interactions; specialist advice Same-day
  • Postpartum confusion, mania or psychosis → perinatal or crisis team Same-day

1Confirm — suspect, then refer

Before any antidepressant, ask about past elevated or irritable mood, overactivity, little need for sleep. No screening questionnaires in primary care.

EpisodeDuration
Mania (may be psychotic)At least 1 week, or admission
HypomaniaAt least 4 days, no psychosis

2Check

☐ Risk: suicide, others, psychosis, not eating or drinking, money, children ☐ Drugs, alcohol, steroids ☐ TFT, FBC, U&E, calcium, HbA1c ☐ Before lithium: weight, eGFR, TFT, calcium, ECG if cardiovascular risk ☐ Pregnancy potential, contraception. Don't miss: organic cause of new mania after 50.

3Decide

  • Never an antidepressant alone; if mania emerges on one, consider stopping it and seek advice.
  • Specialists start antipsychotics and lithium; lithium is first-line long-term, GP continues under shared care.
  • Valproate: not started under 55 without two specialists; Pregnancy Prevention Programme; men — contraception during and 3 months after.

4Target — plasma lithium, 12 h post-dose

SituationTarget
First prescription0.6–0.8 mmol/L
Relapsed on lithium or subthreshold symptomsConsider 0.8–1.0 mmol/L

5Treat — lithium safely

PrescribeBy brand; record book and alert card; same brand every time.
AvoidNSAIDs, ACE inhibitors, ARBs, diuretics — if essential, level and U&E within about a week.
Sick daysVomiting, diarrhoea, fever, heat → fluids; can't keep fluids down → contact same day.
StoppingNever abruptly — over at least 4 weeks with the specialist.
  • Doses: NICE/SPC, BNF, local formulary.

6Monitor

  • Level: 1 week after start or dose change until stable → every 3 months for the first year → every 6 months (3-monthly in older people, interacting drugs, last level 0.8 mmol/L or higher).
  • Every 6 months: weight or BMI, U&E and eGFR, TFT, calcium.
  • Annual physical health check: BP, pulse, HbA1c, lipids, LFTs, smoking, alcohol.

7Refer

  • Hypomania, relapse signs, pregnant or planning pregnancy on lithium or valproate Urgent
  • Suspected bipolar in depression, level persistently out of range, falling eGFR, thyroid or calcium problems Routine

8Never

  • Start an antidepressant alone in bipolar disorder.
  • Start an NSAID, ACE inhibitor or thiazide on lithium without a level plan.
  • Issue lithium without up-to-date monitoring results.
  • Stop lithium abruptly, or stop valproate in pregnancy without specialist advice.
  • Accept a “normal” level as excluding toxicity in a symptomatic patient.

9Safety-net — say it, then document it

999 “Thoughts of ending your life with a plan, or you feel unsafe — call 999, or your crisis team or NHS 111 option 2.”   Same-day “Bad shaking, sickness or diarrhoea, unsteady, slurred or confused on lithium — stop it and get seen today.”   Early signs: “Sleeping less, racing thoughts, spending more — contact your team within a day or two.”   Medicines: “No ibuprofen without checking; tell every prescriber you take lithium.”

✓Safe consultation check — all five done, case safely managed

Risk assessed; red flags excluded Bipolar asked about before any antidepressant Lithium results in date; interactions checked; pregnancy potential Shared-care plan, recall and record book in place Safety-net and crisis plan documented
