Secondary prevention after myocardial infarction

For: adults after STEMI or NSTEMI, from discharge onwards. Not for: chest pain now (999), stable angina without MI (use the stable angina protocol) or full heart-failure therapy (use the heart-failure protocol).
Reviewed October 2026 · next review April 2027
NICE NG185NG238TA733 · TA393 · TA394GMMMG lipid pathwayDVLA
Core rule: ACE inhibitor, antiplatelets, beta-blocker and high-intensity statin unless contraindicated — titrated to target or maximum tolerated dose, not left at starter doses — and never stop DAPT without the cardiology plan.

0Act now — check first, every time

  • Suspected re-infarction — pain like their MI, not gone 5 minutes after a second GTN dose, worse, or unwell → 999
  • Significant bleeding on DAPT — black tarry stools, vomiting blood, a bleed that will not stop → 999 / A&E Interruption is a multidisciplinary decision.
  • New angina or breathlessness → stable angina or a new event? ACS → 999; recurrent angina → cardiology.
  • Asked to stop an antiplatelet for dental work, surgery, endoscopy or preference → check the proceduralist and cardiology plan first: early cessation after stenting risks stent thrombosis.
  • Lorry or bus driver (Group 2) → must stop driving and notify the DVLA.

2Check — first review and every titration

☐ BP, symptoms  ☐ U&E, potassium  ☐ Lipids, transaminases  ☐ HbA1c  ☐ Depression, anxiety, suicide risk  ☐ Rehab attended?  ☐ Licence group

Don't miss: starter doses, or a statin that quietly stopped.

3Decide — core treatment unless contraindicated

  • Everyone: ACE inhibitor · DAPT (unless anticoagulation is indicated) · beta-blocker · high-intensity statin.
  • MRA only if LVEF ≤40% with heart-failure symptoms or signs — via the cardiology plan.
  • Anticoagulated (e.g. AF) → clopidogrel usual; triple therapy time-limited, cardiology-led — record its stop date.
  • Cardiac rehabilitation for everyone — present it as a core treatment.
  • Lifestyle: stop smoking, Mediterranean diet, activity, alcohol, weight; annual flu vaccine.

5Treat — doses per BNF and local formulary

  • DAPT: aspirin 75 mg + the P2Y12 cardiology chose (ticagrelor, prasugrel, clopidogrel) — don't switch it in primary care.
  • Ramipril (ARB if intolerant) and bisoprolol, titrated; never stop a beta-blocker abruptly.
  • Eplerenone only if MRA criteria met; don't swap to spironolactone automatically.
  • PPI if GI bleeding risk (age, prior ulcer, steroids); not omeprazole or esomeprazole with clopidogrel.
LipidsAtorvastatin 80 mg → consider ezetimibe 10 mg if target not met, and even when met
ThenInclisiran or PCSK9 inhibitor only if NICE TA and GMMMG criteria are met

"Intolerant": rechallenge, lower dose, other statin or alternate days — nocebo is common; then GMMMG pathway.

6Monitor

  • ACE inhibitor or MRA change: U&E, potassium, BP before and 1–2 weeks after.
  • Lipids, transaminases: 2–3 months after any change, then at least annually.
  • DAPT stop date diarised → aspirin alone, per the cardiology plan.
  • Annually: angina, heart failure, bleeding, adherence, BP, HbA1c, U&E, LDL, smoking, mood, driving.

7Refer — and driving

  • Cardiology: recurrent angina, DAPT duration, high bleeding risk, triple therapy, device or EF concerns. Heart-failure team: EF ≤40% or heart failure.
  • Lipid clinic: above LDL target on statin plus ezetimibe, or possible familial hypercholesterolaemia.
  • DVLA Group 1: 1 week off after successful PCI with adequate LV function, otherwise 4 weeks. Group 2: stop and notify; relicensing ≥6 weeks if criteria met.

8Never

  • Stop or change either antiplatelet without the proceduralist and cardiology plan.
  • Leave the ACE inhibitor and beta-blocker at starter doses — started is not treated.
  • Accept "statin intolerance" without a rechallenge or an alternative.
  • Routinely combine an ACE inhibitor and an ARB.
  • Let triple therapy run on through repeat-prescription inertia.

9Safety-net — say it, then document it

999 “Chest pain like your heart attack: sit down and use your GTN; still there after 5 minutes, take a second dose; still there 5 minutes after that, worse, or you feel unwell — or no GTN — call 999. Do not drive yourself.”   999 / A&E “Black tarry stools, vomiting blood or a bleed that won't stop — 999 or A&E now.”   DAPT: “Don't stop the blood thinners for dental work or surgery without checking with us or cardiology.”   Feeling well: “Carry on all your heart tablets — that is when they are working.”
