Safeguarding in primary care

For: children, young people and adults at risk seen in general practice, including domestic abuse, FGM, Prevent and modern slavery. Not for: investigating abuse (social care and police) or child protection medicals (specialist paediatric / SARC). Local procedures set the routes.
Reviewed October 2026 · next review April 2027
Working Together 2023NICE CG89 · NG76Care Act 2014Domestic Abuse Act 2021FGM Act s5B
Core rule: if you suspect harm, refer the same day — do not wait for proof, for consent that would increase risk, or for someone else to act; immediate danger means 999.

0Act now — check first, every time

  • Immediate danger, serious assault, perpetrator threatening → 999 (silent caller: 999 then 55).
  • Suspected child maltreatment — any bruise in a non-mobile baby, disclosure, unexplained injury → children’s social care / MASH Same-day
  • Child under 13 sexually active, or sexual abuse suspected → social care and police per local procedure Same-day
  • Known FGM in a girl under 18 (told by her or signs seen) → personal report to police on 101 + social care Same-day (by close of next working day at the latest)
  • High-risk domestic abuse (DASH ≥14, judgement, escalation) → MARAC via IDVA Same-day  Adult at risk, serious harm → adult social care Same-day

1Confirm — suspect, consider or exclude (NICE CG89)

LevelMeaning → action
SuspectSerious concern, not proof → refer to children’s social care
ConsiderOne possible explanation → gather information, discuss with safeguarding lead, review
ExcludeSuitable explanation found → record it

Adult at risk (Care Act s42): care and support needs + abuse or neglect + cannot protect themselves.

2Check

☐ Other children in the household, and unborn babies  ☐ Domestic abuse, substance misuse, parental mental illness  ☐ ‘Was not brought’ pattern  ☐ Capacity for the specific decision  ☐ Coercion, exploitation, someone speaking for the patient

Don’t miss: ask about abuse alone, with a professional interpreter.

3Decide — consent and sharing

  • Seek consent when safe; share without it to protect a child, when others are at risk, for serious crime, lack of capacity or coercion.
  • Gillick: under 16 can consent with sufficient understanding. Fraser criteria for contraception without parents.
  • Under 13 cannot consent to sex; 13–17: assess for exploitation.

5Respond — recognise, respond, refer, record

ListenDo not lead; never promise secrecy; treat injuries; ensure safety now
DiscussSafeguarding lead or named GP — but discussion never delays a referral
ReferPhone, then in writing; check receipt; escalate if concern persists
RecordOwn words, body map, reasoning, who told, reference numbers, codes and alerts
  • Domestic abuse: DASH; IRIS advocate; Helpline 0808 2000 247; safety plan; check the children.
  • FGM ≥18 or at risk: safeguarding, not the mandatory duty; FGM Enhanced Dataset.

6Monitor

  • Review at the next contact: did the referral arrive, what was decided, is the concern resolved?
  • Code and flag child protection plan, looked-after child, MARAC — on the child’s and relevant family records.
  • Conferences and strategy meetings: share promptly; facts and professional opinion.

7Refer — the other routes

  • Allegation against someone working with children → LADO Within 1 working day
  • Radicalisation concern → Prevent lead, local Prevent referral Urgent
  • Modern slavery: adult → NRM via a first responder with consent; child → social care. Helpline 08000 121 700.
  • Early help for emerging needs, with consent.

8Never

  • Wait for proof before referring a suspicion.
  • Ask about abuse with a partner or relative present, or use family as interpreters.
  • Delegate the FGM report or examine to look for FGM.
  • Treat data protection as a reason not to share.
  • See the adult without thinking about the child behind them.

9Safety-net — say it, then document it

999 “If you or the children are in danger, call 999 — if you can’t speak, press 55.”   Domestic abuse: “The National Domestic Abuse Helpline is free, 24 hours: 0808 2000 247.”   Disclosure: “I can’t keep this a secret, but I will tell you who I’m sharing it with and why.”
