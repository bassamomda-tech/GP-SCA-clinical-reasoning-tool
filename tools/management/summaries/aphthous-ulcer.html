Aphthous ulcer (recurrent aphthous stomatitis)

For: mouth ulcers seen in primary care, single or recurrent. Not for: ulceration on chemotherapy, radiotherapy or other anticancer treatment (acute oncology pathway), or an undifferentiated mouth ulcer (use the mouth ulcers pathway).
Reviewed October 2026 · next review April 2027
NICE NG12PCDSBAD/BSR Behçet's (2025)GM formulary & OTC policySPS/MHRA
Core rule: an unexplained oral ulcer lasting more than 3 weeks → suspected cancer pathway referral (NICE: consider; local default: refer) — less pain is not healing, and a topical steroid is never a diagnostic trial.

0Act now — urgent, and not only cancer

  • Airway compromise, stridor or rapidly progressive swelling → 999
  • Possible SJS/TEN (mouth blistering, target rash, skin pain, sore eyes) → stop the drug → Same-day hospital; 999 if critically unwell
  • Unable to maintain hydration or nutrition · severe gingivitis with fever · immunocompromised with extensive ulceration → Same-day
  • On chemotherapy, radiotherapy or other anticancer treatment → follow the Same-day acute oncology pathway (mucositis, neutropenia, infection), not simple aphthosis.
  • New ulcer on methotrexate → withhold the next dose, urgent FBC and renal/liver tests, discuss with the shared-care team. Fever, sore throat, bruising, bleeding, severe mucositis, reduced intake or possible overdose → Same-day
  • Unexplained oral ulcer >3 weeks, or persistent unexplained neck lump → consider Suspected cancer pathway (local default: refer, even if symptoms have improved).
  • Lump on the lip or in the mouth, or red or red-white patch (erythroplakia, erythroleukoplakia) → consider urgent dental referral Within 2 weeks; the dentist refers if suspicious.

1Confirm — examine properly, then classify

Ulcer: site, size, number, duration, edge, base, induration, bleeding. Mouth: red/white patches, ventral tongue, floor, teeth, dentures. Neck nodes; weight loss, dysphagia, otalgia, trismus, voice change.

Patterns, not diagnoses: a presumed major ulcer unexplained >3 weeks still needs referral.

2Check — recurrent, severe or atypical only; selective

☐ FBC  ☐ Ferritin/iron studies  ☐ B12, folate  ☐ Coeliac tTG  ☐ Calprotectin if bowel symptoms  ☐ HIV if risk-based or atypical  ☐ Medicines: nicorandil, NSAIDs, methotrexate, azathioprine, ACEi/ARB

Don't miss Behçet's: genital ulcers, uveitis, skin, vascular, neuro or joint features. Deficiency → investigate its cause, not just replace. No routine HSV swabs.

3Decide — self-care first

  • Uncomplicated minor aphthae → self-care and pharmacy advice; prescribe only when clinically indicated (GM OTC policy; age, frailty, swallowing).
  • Topical steroid → only for clinically diagnosed aphthae with substantial symptoms or frequent episodes.
  • Triggers: SLS-free toothpaste trial, avoid trauma and acidic or spicy food, saline rinses, dentist for sharp teeth or dentures.

5Treat — topical, diagnosed aphthae only

PainBenzydamine 0.15% spray or mouthwash, per SPC (GM: mainly palliative or post-radiotherapy).
Severe painLidocaine gel, oral formulation; no food or drink while numb.
Short courseChlorhexidine 0.2%: painful, recurrent or infected ulcers; apart from toothpaste.
SteroidHydrocortisone 2.5 mg oromucosal tablets or betamethasone 500 microgram mouthwash (off-label; spit out).
  • No steroid if herpes, fungal infection, SJS/TEN or other infection suspected. No chlorhexidine in chlorhexidine allergy (anaphylaxis, MHRA). Doses per BNF and local formulary.

6Monitor — persistent or recurrent? Re-check

  • Each review: fully healed? · deficiency corrected, recurrence reassessed · IBD, Behçet's or drug cause · trauma → dentist.
  • Methotrexate: once weekly · 2.5 mg tablets only · no trimethoprim or co-trimoxazole · NSAIDs, dehydration, renal function · folic acid another day.

7Refer — routine specialist

  • Oral medicine or OMFS: severe, frequent or disabling recurrence; major or complex; recurs despite correcting a deficiency; diagnostic uncertainty. IBD or other systemic cause → relevant specialty.
  • Suspected Behçet's → multispecialty assessment; new red eye, photophobia or visual change → Same-day ophthalmic.

8Never

  • A topical steroid as a trial for an unexplained ulcer persisting beyond 3 weeks.
  • Accept less pain as healing — document whether the lesion has fully resolved.
  • Label a large, solitary, indurated or atypical lesion major aphthosis unreassessed.
  • Trimethoprim or co-trimoxazole with methotrexate, or methotrexate taken daily.
  • Stop essential drugs (e.g. nicorandil) without the prescriber, or start colchicine for Behçet's in primary care.

9Safety-net — say it, then document it

999 “Difficulty breathing or swallowing, or swelling that is quickly getting worse — call 999.”   Same-day “Mouth blisters with a rash, skin pain or sore eyes — A&E today. Unable to eat or drink, a fever, or new ulcers while on methotrexate or cancer treatment — contact us the same day.”   Within a week “If an ulcer hasn't fully healed within 3 weeks — even if it hurts less — or it bleeds, feels hard, keeps getting rubbed by a tooth, or you notice a lump in your neck, contact us to be seen within a week.”   “Smoking and alcohol raise mouth-cancer risk.”
