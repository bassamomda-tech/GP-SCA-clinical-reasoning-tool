Type 2 diabetes in adults

For: adults with new or confirmed type 2 diabetes. Not for: type 1/LADA or pregnancy (specialist teams); use insulin in T2DM, sick day rules, DKA, Ramadan.
Reviewed October 2026 · next review April 2027
NICE NG28 (updated Feb 2026)NG12NG136NG238NG203
Core rule: prove it is type 2 (not type 1, not a pancreatic cancer flag), treat cardiovascular risk as hard as glucose, and give written sick-day rules with every SGLT2 inhibitor.

0Act now — check first, every time

  • Vomiting, abdominal pain, Kussmaul breathing or drowsiness → ketones ≥3.0 mmol/L or clinical concern → 999 / immediate hospital. Glucose may be normal on an SGLT2i — stop it.
  • HHS (severe hyperglycaemia, profound dehydration, altered mental state), glucose <3 mmol/L with confusion on sulfonylurea/insulin, chest pain or FAST+ → 999. Sudden visual loss → Same-day ophthalmology or 999.
  • Hot swollen foot with fever → Same-day diabetic foot team (sepsis → 999); hot swollen foot, no trauma (Charcot?) → Urgent MDT foot service
  • Aged 60+, new diabetes and unexplained weight loss → Urgent direct-access CT abdomen ≤2 weeks (ultrasound if no CT) — pancreatic cancer.
  • Type 1 / LADA features at any age or BMI (ketosis, weight loss, rapid deterioration, low BMI, autoimmunity) → Same-day ketones + diabetes-team advice

2Check — at diagnosis

☐ BMI, BP both arms  ☐ U&E/eGFR (before metformin), ACR, lipids, LFTs  ☐ QRISK3  ☐ Monofilament foot check  ☐ Eye screening (DESP) referral now  ☐ Smoking, mood. ECG, TSH, FBC if indicated.

Symptomatic with glucose >15–20 → ketones the same day.

3Decide — who gets what

  • Needs drugs → offer MR metformin + SGLT2i to most, unless contraindicated or frailty risks volume depletion.
  • Established ASCVD → also subcutaneous semaglutide (≤1 mg weekly), any HbA1c.
  • Meets NHS eligibility → refer to Type 2 Path to Remission; bariatric assessment per NG246.
  • All: DESMOND; ≥150 min/week activity; alcohol <14 units/week; no smoking.

4Target

HbA1c48; 53 on sulfonylurea/insulin; relaxed if frail; intensify ≥58
BP<140/90; 80+ <150/90; CKD + ACR ≥70 <130/80
LipidsQRISK3 ≥10% → atorvastatin 20 mg; CVD → 80 mg
KidneysACR ≥3 + CKD → max tolerated ACEi/ARB; SGLT2i per NICE TA

5Treat — NG28 2026 ladder, branch by phenotype

Step 1 · dualMR metformin 500 mg OD → 1–2 g/day, then add dapagliflozin or empagliflozin 10 mg. No metformin → SGLT2i (DPP-4i if frail)
Step 2 · phenotypeASCVD → + semaglutide. Heart failure → SGLT2i. eGFR 20–30 → SGLT2i + DPP-4i. Obesity, onset <40 → earlier GLP-1 RA/tirzepatide
Step 3 · addGLP-1 RA/tirzepatide, DPP-4i, gliclazide, or pioglitazone (never in heart failure)
Step 4 · insulinSymptoms, weight loss, ketosis — not HbA1c alone → insulin protocol
  • Before SGLT2i: past DKA, dehydration, keto diet? Not if unwell. Sick-day rules in writing.
  • Before every step: adherence, lifestyle, intercurrent cause, right target, right diagnosis.
  • Frail: relax target; stop sulfonylurea/insulin first. Doses per BNF and local formulary.

6Monitor

  • HbA1c + U&E at 3 months, 3–6-monthly, then 6-monthly when stable. <48 on sulfonylurea/insulin → consider de-intensifying.
  • ACEi/ARB: K⁺, creatinine at 1–2 weeks. eGFR <45 → review metformin; <30 or AKI → hold it; AKI 2–3 → Same-day nephrology
  • Annually: 9 care processes incl. foot risk and eye screening; mood, hypos, driving, pregnancy plans. B12 if metformin >4 years.

7Refer — specialist

  • Urgent diabetology suspected type 1/LADA (GAD antibodies first); recurrent severe hypos or lost awareness; pregnancy.
  • Routine diabetology: above target despite triple therapy and injectables considered; suspected MODY.
  • Renal: eGFR <30 or falling >5/year; ACR ≥70 on ACEi/ARB.
  • Tell DVLA: on insulin; Group 2 also on sulfonylurea. “5 to drive.”

8Never

  • Start metformin and “review in 3 months” in someone 60+ with new diabetes and weight loss.
  • Label it type 2 and start metformin when type 1 features are present — it can be fatal.
  • Diagnose on one HbA1c in an asymptomatic person, or on 42–47.
  • Start an SGLT2 inhibitor during intercurrent illness or without written sick-day rules; or pair a DPP-4i with a GLP-1 RA.
  • Continue a statin or ACE inhibitor into a planned pregnancy — stop before conception, folic acid 5 mg, joint diabetes–antenatal clinic; aim HbA1c <48.

9Safety-net — say it, then document it

999 “Vomiting, tummy pain, drowsiness or deep fast breathing — call 999. On a ‘flozin’ tablet this can happen even with normal-ish sugars.”   Sick days: “Can't eat or drink, or vomiting: pause metformin, the ‘flozin’, the ACE inhibitor and any water tablet; keep insulin going; restart when eating and drinking normally.”   Feet: “New ulcer, colour change, spreading redness or a hot swollen foot — call us the same day.”   Hypos: “Shaky, sweaty, confused — 15 g fast sugar, recheck in 15 minutes, then a snack.” Same-day contact if glucose >20 for 48 hours.
