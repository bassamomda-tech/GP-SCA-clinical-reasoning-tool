Postnatal disorders

For: women after birth (sepsis risk window 6 weeks, also after termination, miscarriage or ectopic; mental illness through the first postnatal year) and the 6–8-week GP review. Not for: pregnancy, or detail on one condition — see postnatal depression, mastitis, hypertension in pregnancy, contraception.
Reviewed October 2026 · next review April 2027
NICE NG194CG192NG255NG133RCOG GTG 64 · 37b
Core rule: a postnatal woman who is “not quite right” needs a face-to-face assessment, not telephone reassurance — sepsis, VTE, haemorrhage, pre-eclampsia and psychosis can all look mild early, and fever alone neither rules sepsis in nor out.

0Act now — ask at every postnatal contact

  • Unwell or suspected infection ≤6 weeks after birth → Same-day face-to-face: full obs, mental state, perfusion; early warning score (NG255). High-risk criteria or rapid deterioration → 999 with pre-alert; don't delay for IV antibiotics. Pain out of proportion → necrotising GAS; notify UKHSA HPT.
  • Suspected PE with significant breathlessness, chest pain, hypoxia, collapse or instability → 999. Other suspected DVT/PE → Same-day objective imaging; a normal D-dimer or Wells score never excludes VTE.
  • Psychosis (confusion, grandiosity, labile mood, severe agitation) → immediate specialist perinatal assessment, ≤4 h of referral; 999 if immediate danger. Assess safety of mother and baby; refuses and lacks capacity or poses a risk → urgent Mental Health Act assessment.
  • Severe headache, visual symptoms, epigastric pain, confusion or BP ≥160/110 → Same-day maternity/acute (pre-eclampsia, CVST, bleed, meningitis). Seizure, altered consciousness, suspected eclampsia → 999
  • Secondary PPH: unstable or actively haemorrhaging → 999, major-haemorrhage pathway; stable but abnormal or persistent bleeding → Same-day obstetric
  • Active suicidal ideation, or thoughts of harming the baby with uncertain intent or reality testing → Same-day perinatal mental health; safeguarding referral if actual or likely significant harm, with rationale documented.

1Confirm and decide — mood: clinical, not a score

ConditionClueDecide
Baby bluesTearful days 3–5, gone by 2 weeksReassure; arrange a check
Postnatal depressionLow mood >2 weeks, anhedonia, guilt, poor bondingMild: Talking Therapies. Moderate–severe: SSRI + therapy
Perinatal OCDHarm thoughts she dreads and resistsSSRI + CBT (ERP)
Birth-trauma PTSDIntrusive memories, avoidanceTrauma CBT or EMDR; debrief

EPDS/PHQ-9 supports, never replaces, clinical assessment. Uncertain reality testing → block 0.

2Check — every contact and the 6–8-week review

☐ EPDS/PHQ-9, Q10 asked aloud, harm-to-baby thoughts ☐ Obs, bleeding; look at perineum/wound, breasts, calves ☐ Bladder, bowel, pelvic floor ☐ Domestic abuse, baby's wellbeing

FBC if PPH >500 mL, antenatal anaemia or symptoms; TFTs only if thyroid features. Don't miss: anaemia behind “exhaustion”.

4Targets and thresholds

FindingAction
BP ≥150/100, GH or PE, untreatedStart treatment (NG133); aim <140/90
Hb <100 g/L, stableOral iron 40–80 mg elemental daily; Hb at 2–3 weeks. Bleeding or severe anaemia → block 0

5Treat — breastfeeding is not a veto; doses per BNF and local formulary

SSRI if breastfeedingStarting: sertraline or paroxetine commonly preferred. Don't switch a stable woman. Watch baby: sedation, feeding, weight.
MastitisAnalgesia, rest, cold packs, keep feeding. Bacterial, systemic or no better at 24–48 h → antibiotic per GM guideline.
Postnatal BPEnalapril (U&E, K⁺); Black African or Caribbean origin: nifedipine. Inadequate → combine.
Perineum · continenceLook first. Paracetamol + ibuprofen, saline washes. Pelvic floor physio.

6Monitor and review

  • New antidepressant: review ≤2 weeks (1 week if 18–25 or suicide risk); usually ≥6 months after remission.
  • Contraception: discuss in week 1 — needed from day 21.
  • 6–8-week review: mother and baby; unresolved → named interval and person.

7Refer — urgent routes also in block 0

  • Perinatal mental health: moderate–severe PND, SMI or bipolar, previous psychosis, poor response.
  • Suspected breast abscess → Same-day breast assessment and ultrasound.
  • Baby: projectile or green vomit, jaundice <24 h old (bilirubin ≤2 h) → Same-day; jaundice >14 days (>21 preterm) → pale stools?
  • Maternity: wound breakdown, retained products. Retention → Same-day

8Never

  • Rule out sepsis on a normal temperature, or VTE on a normal D-dimer or Wells score.
  • Score EPDS question 10 silently from a form, or let a score alone decide.
  • Leave depression untreated “because I'm breastfeeding”.
  • Stop lithium or an antipsychotic abruptly (get perinatal psychiatry advice), or manage psychosis at home alone.
  • Delay emergency transfer to give IV antibiotics, or mental-health care to await TFTs.

9Safety-net — say it out loud, then document it

999 “Very unwell or rapidly worse, with or without a temperature; sudden heavy bleeding, large clots or feeling faint; sudden breathlessness or chest pain; a fit, or severe headache with vision changes; sudden confusion, racing thoughts or feeling unable to keep yourself or the baby safe — call 999.”
Same day “Not right or a persisting temperature; wound or tummy more painful, red or discharging; smelly discharge or bleeding increasing again; painful swollen calf; severe headache or visual change; red painful breast not improving; can't pass urine; worsening mood, suicidal or frightening thoughts about the baby — contact us the same day.”   Disclosure: “Telling me frightening thoughts is right — it does not mean anyone will take your baby.”

✓Safe consultation check — all five done, case safely managed
