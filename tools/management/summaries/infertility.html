Infertility

For: people and couples trying to conceive, any partnership status, both partners assessed in primary care. Not for: assisted conception or ovulation induction (fertility services); see the PCOS and erectile dysfunction pages.
Reviewed October 2026 · next review April 2027
NICE NG257 (2026)NICE NG12BASHH PID 2019HFEANHS GM ICB policy
Core rule: investigate both partners in parallel from the first visit, and offer referral at presentation when the person trying to conceive is 36 or over or either partner has a cause — age does not come back.

0Act now — found during the fertility workup

  • Unwell after ovarian stimulation (OHSS: abdominal swelling, pain, vomiting, less urine) → Same-day call to her fertility unit; breathless, chest pain or collapse → 999.
  • Non-painful enlargement or change in shape or texture of a testis → consider a Suspected cancer pathway referral; unexplained or persistent testicular symptoms → consider a direct-access ultrasound.
  • Cervix looks consistent with cancer → consider a Suspected cancer pathway referral, even after recent normal screening.
  • Azoospermia or grossly deficient count → repeat as soon as possible and Refer now; don't wait for the repeat.
  • Acute PID: pregnancy test, exclude ectopic; severe, pregnant, abscess or can't take oral → consider admission or Urgent specialist review.
  • Coercion or non-consensual sex → safeguarding first, asked in private; FGM under 18 → mandatory police report.

1Confirm — investigate now, or reassure and review?

SituationAction
<1 year, under 36, no predictive historyInformation, preconception care, review date
1 year of regular unprotected sexInvestigate both without delay; refer
Aged 36 or over, or predictive history*Offer referral at presentation; tests in parallel
Erectile or ejaculatory problems, no sexAddress first: the likely cause

*Cycle disturbance, PID, ectopic, pelvic surgery, endometriosis; undescended testes, mumps orchitis, testicular surgery or trauma, chemo- or radiotherapy (semen analysis now). Under 40: over 80% conceive in 1 year, over 90% in 2.

2Check — both partners, same visit

☐ Semen analysis ☐ Progesterone 7 days before expected period (day 25 of 32) ☐ Chlamydia before uterine instrumentation ☐ Rubella if unsure ☐ FSH/LH only if cycles irregular ☐ TFTs, prolactin if indicated ☐ HbA1c, BMI, BP, smear ☐ Steroids, testosterone

Don't miss genital and cervical examination. Not routine: tubal, post-coital, antisperm or sperm DNA tests.

3Decide — preconception care primary care owns

  • Folic acid 400 micrograms daily to 12 weeks; 5 mg only if nationally indicated (previous neural-tube defect, diabetes, some antiseizure drugs), not BMI alone.
  • Sex every 2–3 days, not ovulation tracking. Stop smoking, both. Alcohol: none safest for her, within limits for him.
  • BMI toward 20–25; not a precondition for referral.
  • Optimise chronic disease; switch teratogens (valproate, ACE inhibitors) first.

4Target — semen reference limits (NG257)

Volume ≥1.4 mL · ≥16 million/mL · total ≥39 million · motility ≥42% (progressive ≥30%) · vitality ≥54% · morphology ≥4%. Reference limits, not diagnostic.

5Treat — get the semen analysis right

CollectAbstain 2–7 days; to the lab within 1 hour, kept warm
AbnormalExclude artefacts; repeat after 3 months
Two abnormalExamine; consider testosterone, FSH, LH
No ejaculatePost-ejaculatory urine; refer urology
  • No testosterone for male infertility; no routine antioxidants or metformin for fertility.
  • Acute PID (not pregnant): ceftriaxone 1 g IM, then doxycycline 100 mg BD + metronidazole 400 mg BD, 14 days. Doses: NICE/SPC, BNF, local formulary.

6Monitor

  • Low progesterone → repeat, correctly timed, before calling it anovulation; irregular cycles → weekly.
  • Raised prolactin → repeat; consider drugs, pregnancy, thyroid, macroprolactin.
  • Offer counselling; Fertility Network UK; a private appointment each.

7Refer — non-urgent (urgent in block 0)

  • Send what you have; list outstanding tests. Urology: obstruction, varicocele with abnormal semen, absent vas, hypogonadism.
  • Funding: check current NHS Greater Manchester ICB policy; NICE's 3 IVF cycles <40, 1 at 40–41: not an entitlement.
  • Same-sex, single, trans, non-binary → local pathway.

8Never

  • Investigate one partner while the other waits, or delay referral for baseline tests.
  • Wait out the year at 36 or over, or with a predictive history.
  • Day-21 progesterone whatever the cycle length; AMH or FSH to test natural fertility.
  • Testosterone for male infertility; antibiotics for a remote history of PID.
  • Promise IVF cycles, or make weight loss a precondition for referral.

9Safety-net — say it, then document it

999 Same-day “After any fertility treatment, if your tummy swells or hurts, you are being sick or passing much less urine, contact your fertility unit the same day; if you are breathless or have chest pain, call 999.”   Same-day “Pelvic pain with a fever or unusual discharge — contact us the same day.”   Within a week “A new lump, swelling or change in a testicle, or bleeding after sex or between periods — book to see us within a week; do not wait for the year.”   Routine “Come back at a year, or sooner if anything changes.”
