Fatty liver disease (MASLD / ALD)

For: adults with steatosis on imaging, or suspected metabolic or alcohol-related fatty liver. Not for: decompensated or acute liver failure (block 0), or children. Unexplained abnormal LFTs without known steatosis → use the abnormal LFTs pathway.
Reviewed October 2026 · next review April 2027
NICE NG49 (MASLD; updated July 2026)NG50NG238CG115BSG 2018
Core rule: exclude decompensation, stage fibrosis on the local pathway (never treat an indeterminate FIB-4 as low risk), and treat cardiovascular risk — most die of heart disease, so give an indicated statin, don't withhold it.

0Act now — check first, every time

  • Haematemesis or melaena (suspected variceal bleed) → 999
  • Jaundice with coagulopathy (INR >1.5) or encephalopathy (confusion, asterixis) → Same-day decompensation or acute liver failure; seek the precipitant.
  • New ascites (distension, shifting dullness) → Same-day specialist / hospital — do not delay diagnostic paracentesis.
  • RUQ pain + fever + jaundice (cholangitis), or suspected severe alcoholic hepatitis (Maddrey >32) → Same-day
  • Cirrhosis + new weight loss or RUQ pain, rising AFP or new lesion → Urgent hepatology / radiology (local HCC pathway). Upper abdominal mass consistent with an enlarged liver → consider Urgent direct-access USS (NG12).

1Confirm — metabolic, alcohol, or both?

MASLD = steatosis on imaging + ≥1 cardiometabolic risk factor (BMI >25 or waist >94/80 cm, TG >1.7, low HDL, BP ≥130/85, HbA1c ≥5.7% or pre/type 2 diabetes), after considering alcohol and other causes.

2Check — liver screen for everyone

☐ LFTs, FBC, INR, U&E, lipids, HbA1c, TFTs  ☐ HBsAg; HCV antibody (reflex RNA)  ☐ Autoantibodies + immunoglobulins  ☐ Ferritin + transferrin saturation  ☐ Coeliac, alpha-1 antitrypsin, caeruloplasmin where indicated  ☐ USS abdomen  ☐ Drugs, supplements, herbals  ☐ FIB-4

Don't miss: ALT >3× ULN or rising is not simple MASLD — consider acute viral, drug, autoimmune or ischaemic hepatitis.

3Decide — stage fibrosis on the local pathway

FIB-4Meaning → action
<1.30Low risk → primary care; repeat in 2–3 years (local interval)
1.30 to 2.67 or 3.25 (by pathway)Indeterminate → ELF or FibroScan; never manage as low risk
Above upper cut-offHigh risk → hepatology

NG49 ELF ≥10.51 = advanced fibrosis. Over 65: low-risk cut-off ~2.0. No FIB-4 during acute hepatitis; don't mix pathways' cut-offs.

4Target

  • Weight loss: ≥5% less steatosis · ≥7% better MASH · ≥10% less fibrosis.
  • Activity: ≥150 min/week moderate, plus resistance 2–3 times a week.
  • Alcohol: complete abstinence if MASH, fibrosis or ALD.

5Treat — what kills them is cardiovascular

EveryoneWeight loss, Mediterranean diet, cut sugary drinks, exercise; tier 2 or 3 weight service. CV risk (QRISK3 where valid) → statin where indicated
Type 2 diabetesGLP-1 agonist, SGLT2 inhibitor or pioglitazone for their established indications — not as MASLD treatment
Alcohol-relatedStop alcohol. Assess dependence and withdrawal risk → alcohol team; thiamine per local guidance
CirrhosisHepatology; 6-monthly USS ± AFP; varices per NG50; check vaccinations
  • Statins: transaminases <3× ULN are no reason to withhold. Recheck LFTs and lipids at 2–3 months and 12 months; >3× ULN → investigate the cause.
  • Pioglitazone, vitamin E or resmetirom for MASLD: specialist-only. Doses per BNF and local formulary.

6Monitor

  • Low risk: HbA1c, lipids, BP, weight; re-stage at the local interval (NG49: ELF every 3 years). LFTs only when indicated; no routine serial USS.
  • Rising FIB-4 → re-test or refer. Cirrhosis: USS ± AFP every 6 months; annual FBC, LFTs, INR, albumin, creatinine.
  • Rising bilirubin, new or worsening oedema, ALT >3× baseline in known liver disease → ≤48 hours

7Refer — routine

  • Hepatology: high FIB-4 or advanced ELF; discordant tests; ALT persistently >3× ULN without a cause; suspected autoimmune hepatitis; confirmed hepatitis B or C.
  • Indeterminate FIB-4 → ELF or community FibroScan, per the local pathway.
  • Harmful or dependent drinking → community alcohol team; inpatient withdrawal if high risk.

8Never

  • Withhold or stop an indicated statin because of MASLD or ALT <3× ULN.
  • Manage an indeterminate FIB-4 as low risk.
  • Label a marked or rising transaminase (ALT >3× ULN) as uncomplicated MASLD.
  • Unsupervised benzodiazepine detoxification.
  • NSAIDs in cirrhosis, especially with ascites, renal dysfunction or portal hypertension.

9Safety-net — say it, then document it

999 “Vomiting blood or black, tarry stools — call 999.”   Same-day “Yellow skin or eyes, a swelling tummy, new confusion or drowsiness, or tummy pain with a fever — get seen the same day.”   Statin: “It is safe in fatty liver; I won't stop it for a slightly abnormal liver test.”   Weight: “Losing about a tenth of your weight is the one thing proven to reverse liver scarring.”
