Lipids and hypercholesterolaemia

For: adults with raised lipids or needing CVD lipid modification. Not for: children (FH → lipid clinic), pregnancy (statins stopped; specialist advice), or acute chest pain (999).
Reviewed October 2026 · next review April 2027
NICE NG238CG71 (FH)TA385/393/394/694/733QRISK3 · Simon Broome · DLCNGM lipid pathways
Core rule: spot established CVD and FH before reaching for QRISK3, start atorvastatin at the dose for the patient's group, and recheck at 2–3 months — target missed → adherence, up-titrate, then ezetimibe.

0Act now — check first, every time

  • Acute, ongoing or recent (last 12 hours) chest pain → 999 ACS until proven otherwise, whatever the lipids.
  • Triglycerides ≥10 mmol/L with abdominal pain, nausea or vomiting → Same-day assessment for pancreatitis; consider admission.
  • On a statin: severe muscle pain or weakness, or dark urine → stop the statin; CK, renal function and urine Same-day. CK >10× ULN or suspected rhabdomyolysis → Urgent assessment
  • Nephrotic picture (proteinuria +++, low albumin, oedema) → Urgent assessment; AKI, severe oedema, thrombosis or systemic illness → Same-day admission
  • Triglycerides >20 mmol/L or eruptive xanthomata → Urgent specialist. Asymptomatic ≥10 → treat causes, repeat fasting ≤2 weeks, specialist advice; no admission for the result alone.

Assess for FH: TC >7.5 or LDL >4.9 (adult), tendon xanthomata, own or first-degree relative CHD <60 — Simon Broome or DLCN. TC >9 or non-HDL >7.5 → specialist even without family history.

2Check — before a statin; treat secondary causes

☐ Renal function  ☐ ALT/AST  ☐ HbA1c where appropriate  ☐ TSH if thyroid features  ☐ Alcohol, drugs (ciclosporin, amiodarone, retinoids)  ☐ BP, BMI, xanthomata, arcus

CK only if symptoms or myopathy risk; >5× ULN → repeat in 7 days, don't start if still >5×; raised but <5× → start at a lower dose (NG238). Simvastatin + CCBs, macrolides or azoles → dose caps; hold statins during clarithromycin or erythromycin. Don't delay treatment in established CVD.

3Decide — who gets a statin

  • Established CVD → atorvastatin 80 mg at diagnosis (lower for interactions, frailty, CKD, preference).
  • QRISK3 ≥10% → offer atorvastatin 20 mg after informed discussion. <10% → lifestyle; statin not routine but not ruled out.
  • CKD (eGFR <60 or ACR ≥3) → atorvastatin 20 mg. Type 1 diabetes >40, >10 years, nephropathy or risk factors → offer 20 mg.
  • Aged ≥85 → consider atorvastatin 20 mg. HIV ≥40 → offer a statin (BHIVA); check antiretrovirals.
  • Lifestyle alongside, never instead: diet, ≥150 min/week activity, weight, alcohol <14 units, smoking.

5Treat — target missed at 2–3 months

Step 1 · optimiseAdherence, lifestyle, secondary causes; recheck 2–3 months after each step
Step 2 · maximiseAtorvastatin 20 → 40 → 80 mg, or rosuvastatin 10 → 40 mg
Step 3 · addEzetimibe 10 mg on maximum tolerated statin
Step 4 · NICE + GM pathwayBempedoic acid + ezetimibe (statin intolerant); inclisiran (CVD, LDL ≥2.6); PCSK9i via lipid clinic
  • Aches, CK <5× ULN: fix vitamin D, thyroid, interactions; stop 2–4 weeks → rechallenge lower, switch or alternate-day. Failed 2–3 statins → ezetimibe alone. Doses: NICE/SPC, BNF, local formulary.

6Monitor

  • Lipids + ALT/AST 2–3 months after any change; ALT/AST at 12 months, then if indicated. Stop only if persistently >3× ULN.
  • CK only with symptoms; 5–10× ULN → stop, seek advice. Annually: consider a medicines review (adherence, lifestyle) and a non-fasting non-HDL test.
  • Untreated, QRISK <10%: reassess in 5 years, sooner if risk changes.

7Refer — lipid clinic, per local GM pathway

  • Promptly: possible or probable FH (genetic testing, cascade); homozygous FH (LDL >13 aged ≥16, >11 under 16) directly.
  • Routine: above target on maximum statin + ezetimibe; ≥3 statins not tolerated; complex mixed dyslipidaemia.

8Never

  • Put FH or established CVD through QRISK3.
  • A statin in pregnancy — stop about 3 months before conception, not restarted until breastfeeding ends; discuss contraception.
  • Leave a secondary-prevention patient on nothing because of myalgia or a mild (<3× ULN) transaminase rise.
  • Routine CK when asymptomatic, lipids in acute illness, or a repeat within 3 months.
  • Plant sterols or stanols as CVD prevention.

9Safety-net — say it, then document it

999 “Chest pain means 999, regardless of your cholesterol treatment.”   Same-day “Severe unexplained muscle pain or weakness, dark cola-coloured urine, or yellow skin or eyes — stop the statin and contact us the same day.”   Mild aches: “Tell us rather than stopping.”   Pregnancy: “Tell us if planning a pregnancy; if you become pregnant, stop and contact us.”   FH: “Relatives need testing via the family service.”
