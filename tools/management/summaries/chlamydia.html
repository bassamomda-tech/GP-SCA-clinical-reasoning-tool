Chlamydia

For: genital, rectal or pharyngeal chlamydia in people aged 16 and over in general practice, including pregnancy and breastfeeding. Not for: under-16s (BASHH children and young people guideline + local safeguarding), gonorrhoea (use the gonorrhoea protocol), suspected LGV or undifferentiated urethritis (sexual health).

Core rule: exclude PID, ectopic pregnancy and torsion first; then doxycycline for 7 days (not in pregnancy), no sex until partner management is complete, and book the retest before they leave.

0Act now — any of these changes the plan today

  • Pelvic pain + cervical motion, uterine or adnexal tenderness (PID) → pregnancy test; treat empirically Same-day without waiting for NAATs. Unwell, pregnant, can't take oral treatment, abscess or diagnostic doubt → Same-day admission / specialist advice
  • Positive pregnancy test with pain and abdominal, pelvic or cervical motion tenderness → Immediate EPAU; haemodynamically unstable → 999 / ED
  • Sudden, severe testicular pain → torsion until proven otherwise → Immediate urology / ED. Epididymo-orchitis only once torsion is excluded.
  • Conjunctivitis in an infant under 30 days → Same-day paediatrics / ophthalmology; test mother and partner(s).
  • STI in a child under 13, or a young person at risk of sexual exploitation or abuse → Same-day urgent safeguarding (designated lead, children's social care).

1Confirm — NAAT on the right sample

Usually silent. Chlamydia + gonorrhoea NAATs, usually one specimen.

WhoSample
VaginaVulvovaginal swab (self-taken fine)
PenisFirst-void urine; urethral discharge → sexual health for microscopy + culture
Anal / oral sexRectal swab if indicated; pharyngeal mainly for gonorrhoea

Exposure <2 weeks ago, negative → repeat 2 weeks after exposure if concern remains.

2Check — who to test and what else

☐ Women under 25: offer annually and after a new partner (NCSP); anyone with symptoms, risk or an infected partner ☐ Pregnancy possible? ☐ Exposure sites, partners, condoms ☐ HIV + syphilis (hepatitis by risk) ☐ Coercion, exploitation; under 16 → Fraser + safeguarding

Don't miss: a positive gonorrhoea NAAT on the same sample — doxycycline alone is not enough.

3Decide — treat and stop the chain

  • Positive NAAT → treat; start partner notification at the same visit (sexual health or trained clinician; provider notification if preferred).
  • Contacts: symptomatic, or asymptomatic within 2 weeks of exposure → usually epidemiological treatment after testing.
  • No vaginal, anal or oral sex until course done, symptoms gone and partner management complete.
  • Won't tell partner → explore why; fear of violence or coercion is a safeguarding matter.

5Treat — BASHH 2026

First line (not pregnant)Doxycycline 100 mg twice daily for 7 days — all sites. Single-dose azithromycin no longer routine.
PregnantAzithromycin 1 g, then 500 mg daily for 2 days (preferred); or erythromycin or amoxicillin per BASHH — confirm with sexual health. Doxycycline generally avoided.
BreastfeedingBASHH regimen; doxycycline may be considered if alternatives unsuitable — watch infant for diarrhoea, thrush.
Can't take tetracyclineAlternative agreed with sexual health; azithromycin caution: QT, arrhythmia, liver.
  • Doxycycline: full glass of water, stay upright; separate from antacids, iron, calcium, magnesium, zinc; sun protection.
  • PID: ceftriaxone 1 g IM once + doxycycline 100 mg twice daily + metronidazole 400 mg twice daily for 14 days; review within 72 h if not improving. Doses per BNF and local formulary.

6Monitor — two different tests

  • Retest (reinfection): everyone under 25 → book at about 3 months before they leave; consider at any age with ongoing risk.
  • Test of cure ≥3 weeks after treatment: always in pregnancy; also persistent symptoms, poor adherence, untreated partner, non-standard regimen.

7Refer — non-urgent

  • Sexual health, this week: gonorrhoea; urethritis; rectal symptoms or LGV; doxyPEP; MSM; recurrent positives.
  • Maternity team: every treated pregnancy — document treatment and test-of-cure date.
  • Rheumatology: reactive arthritis persisting after treatment.

8Never

  • Wait for a NAAT to treat suspected PID — a negative test does not exclude it.
  • Treat gonorrhoea, PID or suspected LGV with doxycycline alone.
  • Give doxycycline in pregnancy without specialist advice, or skip the test of cure.
  • Diagnose epididymo-orchitis in sudden testicular pain before torsion is excluded.
  • Treat the patient and leave partner notification undone.

9Safety-net — say it, then document it

999 / A&E “Sudden severe pain in a testicle, or severe tummy pain — especially if you could be pregnant — go to A&E straight away or call 999.”   Same-day “Pain low in your tummy, pain during sex, a fever, or a painful swollen testicle — contact us or the sexual health clinic the same day.”   Partner and retest: “No sex until your partner is treated; I'll book a repeat test in about three months.”   Doxycycline: “Take with a full glass of water, sitting or standing; use sun protection.”
