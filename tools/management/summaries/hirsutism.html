Hirsutism

For: women with excess terminal hair in androgen-dependent areas. Not for: androgen signs before puberty (refer every child — precocious puberty pathway), hypertrichosis (treat the cause), or PCOS subfertility (use the PCOS and infertility pages).
Reviewed October 2026 · next review April 2027
Society for Endocrinology 2025Endocrine Society 2018International PCOS guideline 2023NICE NG12MHRA · FSRH UKMEC
Core rule: ask “how quickly did this come on?” — gradual since puberty is usually PCOS or idiopathic; rapid onset or virilisation is a tumour until proven otherwise and needs urgent referral, never a six-month pill trial.

0Act now — check first, every time

  • Rapid onset over weeks to months, or virilisation (deep voice, clitoromegaly, muscle bulk, temporal balding, breast atrophy) → urgent testosterone + DHEAS → Urgent endocrine / gynae No hormonal trial.
  • Testosterone around or above 5 nmol/L (confirm by LC-MS/MS), markedly raised DHEAS or new androgen excess after the menopause → Urgent endocrine / gynae A lower level does not exclude a tumour.
  • Ascites, or a pelvic or abdominal mass not obviously fibroids → Urgent NG12 ovarian referral
  • Cushing's features (proximal weakness, bruising, plethora, wide purple striae) → overnight dexamethasone test or late-night salivary cortisol → Urgent endocrinology
  • Suspected severe insulin-resistance syndrome (marked acanthosis, very high BMI, young onset) → HbA1c, lipids, BP → Urgent endocrinology
  • Self-harm or severe distress about appearance → ask directly; assess mood and risk Same-day

1Confirm — hirsutism, not hypertrichosis

Hirsutism = terminal (coarse, pigmented) hair in androgen-dependent areas. Fine or generalised hair = hypertrichosis (minoxidil, ciclosporin, phenytoin, anorexia, hypothyroidism) → treat the cause.

Modified Ferriman-Gallwey (9 areas, 0–4 each): ≥4–6 by ethnicity; unreliable after recent removal. Distress below it still warrants treatment.

2Check — a short targeted screen

☐ Morning early-follicular testosterone + SHBG (FAI)  ☐ HbA1c, lipids, BP, BMI if PCOS  ☐ Drugs: anabolic steroids, partner's testosterone gel, danazol, valproate  ☐ Pelvic and abdominal exam

Only if indicated: prolactin, TSH, DHEAS, 17-OHP, cortisol. On a CHC, androgens mislead.

Don't miss: 40+ with persistent bloating, early satiety, pelvic pain or urinary symptoms → CA-125 (NG12 age thresholds).

3Decide — shared decision

  • Gradual, no virilisation or marked androgen excess → manage here.
  • Cosmetic removal is legitimate treatment, from the start, and continues: existing hair does not go. Laser is usually private.
  • Pregnancy plans decide the choice. Weight support is never a precondition.

4Target — when to judge it (say so before prescribing)

Hormonal / anti-androgenAbout 6 months; full effect 9–12 months
Eflornithine creamAbout 8 weeks; stop if no benefit at 4 months

5Treat

Step 1Consider a CHC if UKMEC-eligible
Step 2Co-cyprindiol second-line (higher VTE risk)
Step 3 · inadequate at 6 monthsAdd spironolactone 50–100 mg OD (off-label), only with contraception
Specialist onlyFinasteride, flutamide, higher-dose cyproterone
  • Face: eflornithine 11.5% BD, ≥8 h apart. Metformin if metabolic features dominate.
  • Cyproterone: not with current or past meningioma (MHRA). Doses per BNF and GM formulary.

6Monitor

  • Spironolactone: K⁺, renal function, BP at baseline and after each rise (SPS).
  • 3 months: side effects, contraception, mood — not efficacy. 6 months: repeat the score; escalate if inadequate.
  • <1 bleed every 3–4 months, no CHC → withdrawal bleed, CHC or LNG-IUS.
  • Annually: BMI, BP, HbA1c, lipids if PCOS; UKMEC on a CHC.

7Refer — routine (urgent in block 0)

  • Endocrinology: raised 17-OHP, uncertainty, failure at adequate duration.
  • Fertility: PCOS subfertility — stop the anti-androgen first. Talking Therapies for distress.

8Never

  • A six-month pill trial for rapid onset or virilisation.
  • An anti-androgen when pregnancy is possible without effective contraception, discussed and documented.
  • Judging hormonal treatment before about 6 months.
  • Reading androgens taken on a CHC as reassuring, or screening for Cushing's without clinical features.
  • Co-cyprindiol first-line.

9Safety-net — say it, then document it

Within a few days “Voice deepening, faster hair growth, changes in muscles, breasts or genitals, new hair after the menopause, or new bloating or pelvic pain — contact us within a few days.”   Spironolactone: “Use contraception; might be pregnant — stop it and tell me.”   Expectations: “Judge the tablets at six months. Shaving does not thicken hair.”

✓Safe consultation check — all five done, case safely managed

Pace asked; virilisation and pelvic mass excluded Hirsutism (not hypertrichosis); baseline score recorded Drugs, targeted bloods; pregnancy plans and contraception Six-month rule explained; 3- and 6-month reviews booked Mood asked; safety-net given and documented
