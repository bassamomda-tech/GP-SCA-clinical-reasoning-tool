Long COVID (post-COVID-19 syndrome)

For: adults, children and young people with symptoms 4 weeks or more after acute COVID-19. Not for: acute COVID-19 (see the antivirals protocol) or anyone with red flags (block 0); children with significant impact go to paediatric services.
Reviewed October 2026 · next review April 2027
NICE NG188 (updated 2024)NICE NG206NICE NG225NHS England post-COVID services
Core rule: long COVID is a diagnosis made after other causes are considered; normal tests do not rule it out, and new symptoms are never assumed to be part of it without assessment.

0Act now

  • Severe hypoxaemia, desaturation on exertion or severe lung disease → Same-day; 999 if critically unwell.
  • Cardiac chest pain → 999   Suspected pulmonary embolism → Same-day Wells assessment (NICE NG158).
  • Child with persistent fever, rash, conjunctivitis, abdominal pain or shock (multisystem inflammatory syndrome) → Same-day paediatrics; 999 if shocked.
  • Suicidal thoughts with plan or intent → Same-day crisis assessment (NICE NG225).
  • New focal neurological signs → Same-day or urgent assessment by severity. Weight loss, haemoptysis, lymphadenopathy → investigate; suspected cancer pathway referral if NG12 criteria met.

1Confirm

StageDefinition
Ongoing symptomatic COVID-19Symptoms 4 to 12 weeks after onset
Post-COVID-19 syndromeMore than 12 weeks, not explained by another diagnosis

A positive test is not needed. Symptoms fluctuate and can affect any system.

2Check — guided by symptoms

☐ FBC, kidney and liver function, CRP, ferritin, BNP, thyroid function, HbA1c  ☐ Saturation and 1-minute sit-to-stand test  ☐ Lying and standing BP and heart rate  ☐ ECG if chest pain or palpitations  ☐ PHQ-9, GAD-7. Chest X-ray by 12 weeks if respiratory symptoms continue.

3Decide

  • Post-exertional symptom exacerbation (worse 12 to 48 hours after activity) → pacing; no fixed incremental exercise. Meets ME/CFS criteria → NICE NG206.
  • Significant impact on daily life, or uncertain diagnosis → refer to a post-COVID service from 4 weeks.

4Treat — no specific drug for long COVID

  • Self-management: find a baseline, balance activity and rest, avoid boom and bust.
  • Symptoms: breathing techniques and rehabilitation; postural symptoms: fluids, salt where safe, compression garments; sleep hygiene; pain under NICE NG193.
  • Mood: treat anxiety, depression and post-traumatic stress under NICE guidance.
  • Medicines: do not prescribe drugs or supplements for long COVID outside a trial; treat what you find.

5Monitor

  • Each review: function, post-exertional symptoms, mood, work or school.
  • New or changing symptoms → consider other causes again.
  • Work: phased return, fit note with adjustments, occupational health, Equality Act.

6Refer — non-emergency

  • Adults → integrated multidisciplinary post-COVID service (local criteria).
  • Children → paediatric services or regional paediatric post-COVID hub.
  • Specific complications → respiratory, cardiology, neurology, mental health or ENT.

7Never

  • Put new chest pain, breathlessness or weight loss down to long COVID without assessment.
  • Tell someone normal tests mean nothing is wrong.
  • Advise pushing through post-exertional symptoms.
  • Miss desaturation on exertion.
  • Prescribe unproven drugs or supplements for long COVID.

8Safety-net — say it, then document it

999 “Heavy or crushing chest pain, or struggling to breathe — call 999.”   Same day “More breathless, oxygen below 94%, coughing blood, new weakness or numbness, or thoughts of harming yourself — contact us the same day.”   “If symptoms change or new ones appear, book a review.”
