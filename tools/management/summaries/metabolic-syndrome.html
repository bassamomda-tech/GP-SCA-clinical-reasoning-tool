Metabolic syndrome

For: adults with clustered cardiometabolic risk factors in primary care. Not for: established type 2 diabetes, CVD or suspected FH (use those pathways), or children and adolescents (paediatric services).
Reviewed October 2026 · next review April 2027
IDF / harmonised 2009NICE NG238NG136NG28 (Feb 2026)NG246
Core rule: a risk state, not a disease — reclassify diabetes, CVD, FH and secondary causes first, then treat every component to target.

0Act now — two numbers are easy to file as “another component”

  • Chest pain, breathlessness or stroke symptoms (FAST) → 999
  • Clinic BP ≥180/120 with papilloedema, retinal haemorrhage, confusion, chest pain, heart failure or AKI → Same-day specialist Without → assess target-organ damage, Review ≤7 days
  • Hyperglycaemia: ketotic, unwell or marked symptoms → ketones, Same-day assessment Classic symptoms → Urgent diabetes pathway Asymptomatic HbA1c ≥48 → repeat to confirm.
  • Triglycerides >20 mmol/L (not alcohol or poor glycaemic control) → Urgent specialist lipid review (pancreatitis risk). 10–20 → repeat fasting after 5 days to 2 weeks, seek specialist advice if still >10.
  • Aged 60+ with weight loss and diarrhoea, back or abdominal pain, nausea, vomiting, constipation or new-onset diabetes → consider Urgent direct-access CT ≤2 weeks (pancreas). Unexplained loss >5% in 6 months → urgent investigation or suspected-cancer / non-specific pathway.

1Confirm — reclassify first, then define the cluster

Reclassify: HbA1c ≥48 → diabetes · prior MI, angina, stroke/TIA, claudication → secondary prevention · TC >7.5 with xanthomata or premature CVD → FH pathway · secondary causes only if suggested.

IDF: central obesity plus ≥2 others. Harmonised: ≥3 of 5.

2Check — measure each component

☐ Weight, BMI, ethnicity-adjusted waist  ☐ BP; clinic 140/90–180/120 → ABPM (HBPM if unsuitable)  ☐ HbA1c or fasting glucose  ☐ Lipids incl. LDL, non-HDL, triglycerides  ☐ U&E, LFTs  ☐ QRISK3 (primary prevention only)  ☐ ACR/eGFR if diabetes, hypertension or CKD  ☐ ECG only in confirmed hypertension or if symptomatic

Don't miss: MASLD → FIB-4 then ELF/elastography (normal LFTs don't exclude fibrosis); snoring + sleepiness → Epworth, sleep pathway.

3Decide — treat each component, not the label

  • Everyone: individual weight goal, Mediterranean-style diet, ≥150 min/week + 2 resistance sessions, alcohol ≤14 units, stop smoking.
  • HbA1c 42–47 → refer to NHS Diabetes Prevention Programme where eligible.
  • QRISK3 ≥10% → offer atorvastatin 20 mg, aiming for >40% non-HDL reduction; below 10%, do not exclude if preferred or risk underestimated.
  • Hypertension confirmed by ABPM/HBPM (clinic ≥140/90) → treat to NG136 targets by age, diabetes and CKD.

5Treat — lifestyle is the foundation of every step

GlycaemiaDiabetic → NG28: MR metformin + SGLT2i, added sequentially; GMMMG where it differs
LipidsAtorvastatin 20 mg primary; 80 mg secondary prevention unless not tolerated
Blood pressureT2DM → ACEi or ARB. No diabetes, 55+ or Black African / Caribbean → CCB
WeightOrlistat, or semaglutide / liraglutide within TA875 / TA664
  • Bariatric: offer referral at BMI ≥40, or 35–39.9 with a related condition (consider 2.5 lower in specified ethnic groups); new T2DM → offer expedited assessment ≥35, consider 30–34.9.
  • SGLT2i: check eGFR; warn of genital infection, volume depletion, euglycaemic DKA; sick-day rules.
  • Agree antipsychotic changes with the mental-health team. Doses: NICE/SPC, BNF, local formulary.

6Monitor — no single annual "metabolic syndrome" review

  • Statin: lipids + transaminases at 2–3 months after a change; transaminases at 12 months.
  • Impaired glucose regulation: re-check HbA1c; re-refer to the DPP as needed.
  • Assemble the review from component pathways: ACR/eGFR if diabetes, hypertension or CKD; U&E on antihypertensives; QRISK3; fibrosis per MASLD pathway.

7Refer — routine specialist

  • Tier 3/4 weight service: complex obesity, drug initiation, bariatric assessment.
  • Hepatology: ELF ≥10.51, cirrhosis, portal hypertension, uncertainty (high FIB-4 → second-line test first).
  • Lipid clinic: possible FH, triglycerides persistently >10, statin intolerance. Sleep: suspected OSA. Endocrine: Cushing's, difficult diabetes, PCOS.

8Never

  • Delay treating a component while establishing the label.
  • Use QRISK3 in established CVD or FH.
  • Diagnose FH from total cholesterol alone.
  • Treat the 130/85 clustering cut-off as hypertension, or combine an ACE inhibitor with an ARB.
  • Call weight loss a lifestyle success without asking whether it was intended.

9Safety-net — say it, then document it

999 “Chest pain, sudden breathlessness or stroke symptoms — face drooping, arm weakness, slurred speech — call 999.”   Same-day “Very thirsty, passing lots of urine or losing weight without trying — contact us the same day. Also vomiting, drowsy or very unwell — get same-day help, NHS 111 if we are closed.”   SGLT2 inhibitor: “If you are unwell, vomiting or not eating, pause it and contact us.”
