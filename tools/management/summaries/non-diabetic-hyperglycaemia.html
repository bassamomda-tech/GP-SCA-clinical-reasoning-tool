Non-diabetic hyperglycaemia (prediabetes)

For: adults with HbA1c 42–47 mmol/mol or fasting glucose 5.5–6.9 mmol/L, and women after gestational diabetes. Not for: diabetes (NICE NG28), pregnancy (antenatal pathway) or children (same-day paediatrics).
Reviewed October 2026 · next review April 2027
NICE PH38 (2017)NICE NG28NICE NG3NHS DPPQOF 2026/27
Core rule: explain high risk without alarm, refer to the NHS Diabetes Prevention Programme, and recall for HbA1c and weight every year.

0Act now — when it is not prediabetes

  • Thirst, polyuria, weight loss, ketones, vomiting (possible type 1 or DKA) → Same-day assessment; drowsy or very unwell → 999
  • Child with raised glucose → Same-day paediatrics
  • New hyperglycaemia + weight loss, aged 60 or over → CT for pancreatic cancer per NICE NG12

1Confirm

TestNDHDiabetes
HbA1c42–47 mmol/mol48 or more (repeat if no symptoms)
Fasting glucose5.5–6.9 mmol/L7.0 or more

HbA1c unreliable: pregnancy, children, haemoglobinopathy, anaemia, CKD 4–5, HIV treatment, steroids.

2Check

☐ Symptoms ☐ BMI ☐ Blood pressure ☐ Lipids and QRISK3 ☐ eGFR ☐ Smoking, alcohol ☐ Previous gestational diabetes

Don't miss: code it, so recall happens.

3Decide

  • Offer NHS DPP referral (18+, not pregnant, no diabetes, recent qualifying result).
  • Lifestyle: weight loss, 150 minutes/week activity, fibre, less sugar, alcohol and smoking.
  • Statin and blood pressure per NICE NG238.

5Treat

1 ProgrammeHealthier You NHS DPP, about 9 months
2 LifestylePersonalised goals; obesity services if eligible
3 CVD riskQRISK3, statin, blood pressure
4 MetforminClinical judgement if glucose still rising; off-label; stop if no benefit after 6–12 months

Doses per BNF and local formulary.

6Monitor

  • Every year: HbA1c (same test) and weight or BMI.
  • QOF NDH003: annual test for NDH or previous gestational diabetes.
  • HbA1c 48 or more → confirm and manage as type 2 diabetes.

7After gestational diabetes

  • Annual HbA1c; 39–47 mmol/mol = high risk → lifestyle and NHS DPP.

8Never

  • Call it 'borderline diabetes' and leave it uncoded.
  • Use HbA1c in pregnancy, children or when it is unreliable.
  • Start metformin before lifestyle support has been offered.
  • Ignore cardiovascular risk.
  • Miss type 1 diabetes in a symptomatic person.

9Safety-net

Same-day "If you get very thirsty, pass a lot of urine, lose weight without trying or feel very tired, contact us the same day for a blood test."   "Otherwise we'll check your blood sugar and weight every year."
