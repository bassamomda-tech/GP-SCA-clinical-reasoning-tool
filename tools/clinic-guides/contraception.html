!Act todayCoSRH · NICE NG126 · Fraser

  • Suspected ectopic: positive test or late period with pain, bleeding, shoulder-tip pain or collapse — think of it in any pregnancy with an IUD/IUS in situ or on the POP → same-day early pregnancy assessment; 999 if unstable.
  • VTE or stroke symptoms on CHC (unilateral calf pain or swelling, chest pain, breathlessness, haemoptysis, new aura or focal neurology) → stop the oestrogen, same-day / 999. Severe pain with fever after a coil fitting → perforation or infection.
  • Under 13: cannot consent to sex → safeguarding referral always. Under 16: assess competence and document the Fraser guidelines; ask about age of partner, coercion and exploitation. Undiagnosed abnormal bleeding → investigate before hormones.

1What to ask and measureUKMEC 2025

  • Why now: new start, switch, EC needed today? LMP, UPSI dates
  • Medical history for UKMEC: IHD, stroke/TIA, valve disease, liver, diabetes complications, SLE/antiphospholipid, CKD
  • VTE: personal history, VTE in a first-degree relative, thrombogenic mutation, immobility, surgery
  • Migraine — with or without aura?
  • Smoking or vaping and age ≥35 · BP and BMI recorded before any CHC
  • Breast cancer (current or past) · breastfeeding / weeks postpartum
  • Drugs: enzyme inducers (incl. St John's wort), lamotrigine, valproate, topiramate
  • STI risk · cervical screening · fertility plans · bleeding and hormone preferences

2CHC: UKMEC 4 — do not useUKMEC 2025

  • Migraine with aura (any age) · current or past VTE · known thrombogenic mutation · antiphospholipid antibodies
  • BP ≥160/100 · IHD, stroke/TIA · complicated valvular disease
  • Age ≥35 and ≥15 cigarettes/day (vaping counts as smoking) · <6 weeks postpartum and breastfeeding
  • Current breast cancer (all hormonal methods UKMEC 4) · major surgery with prolonged immobilisation · severe (decompensated) cirrhosis, hepatocellular adenoma or hepatoma

UKMEC 3: BMI ≥35 (extra VTE risk factors → overall clinical risk assessment, UKMEC 2025 multiple-risk-factors framework) · controlled hypertension or 140–159/90–99 · ≥35 and <15/day or stopped <1 year.

Migraine without aura: UKMEC 2 to start; UKMEC 3 to continue if it develops on CHC (reassess, usually switch).

3Exclude pregnancy · Quick StartCoSRH

Reasonably certain not pregnant if no symptoms or signs and any one of:

  • no sex since the start of the last normal period · correct, consistent use of a reliable method
  • days 1–5 of a normal period · <21 days postpartum (not breastfeeding)
  • fully breastfeeding, amenorrhoeic and <6 months postpartum
  • ≤5 days after abortion, miscarriage, ectopic or uterine evacuation
  • no UPSI for >21 days and a negative high-sensitivity urine test

Not certain but ongoing risk → offer EC if indicated, quick-start a pill or implant today (no harm to an early pregnancy) and repeat the test ≥21 days after the last UPSI. No IUD/IUS unless pregnancy is excluded (Cu-IUD as EC within its window).

"Extra cover" = condoms or abstinence. Postpartum: POP/implant any time; CHC not before 21 days (6 weeks if breastfeeding); IUC within 48 h or from 4 weeks.

8Switching without a gapCoSRH

  • COC → POP: start the day after the last active pill, no break → no extra cover. POP → COC: 7 days' cover.
  • To implant, IUS or DMPA: keep the old method going until the new one is effective (box 7).
  • Off a coil: no UPSI in the 7 days before removal, or start the new method first.

9Missed pillsCoSRH CEU (COC) · CoSRH POP

COC (standard 21/7 monophasic EE pills; other regimens per SPC):

  • 1 pill missed (24–48 h late): take it now, carry on. No extra cover.
  • ≥2 missed (>48 h): take the latest now, carry on, condoms until 7 consecutive pills. Week 1 + UPSI in the break or week 1 → consider EC. Week 3 → finish the pack and omit the break.

POP: missed if >3 h late (traditional), >12 h (desogestrel), >24 h (drospirenone). Take one now, carry on; condoms for 2 days (7 days drospirenone). UPSI since the miss and before cover is back → consider EC.

10Emergency contraceptionCoSRH EC (amended Apr 2026)

1 · Cu-IUDMost effective. Within 120 h of the first UPSI or 5 days after earliest expected ovulation, whichever is later. Offer to all.
2 · UPA 30 mgUp to 120 h. Not with an enzyme inducer (or in the last 28 days).
3 · LNG 1.5 mgUp to 72 h (licensed). 3 mg if BMI >26, weight >70 kg or enzyme inducer.
  • After UPA: wait 5 days before starting any progestogen method, then usual extra cover. Progestogen taken in the last 7 days may blunt UPA → LNG or Cu-IUD.
  • After LNG: start hormonal contraception straight away, with usual extra cover.
  • Breastfeeding: no interruption needed after a single UPA dose; LNG and Cu-IUD compatible.
  • Pregnancy test ≥21 days after the last UPSI. Offer STI testing.

11Drug interactionsCoSRH · MHRA

  • Enzyme inducers (carbamazepine, phenytoin, rifampicin, St John's wort, some antiretrovirals): avoid relying on CHC, POP or implant → use Cu-IUD, LNG-IUD or DMPA. Induction lasts 28 days after stopping.
  • Non-enzyme-inducing antibiotics do not reduce efficacy. Lamotrigine: CHC lowers its levels — prefer non-oestrogen methods.
  • Valproate, topiramate (MHRA PPP): highly effective contraception — user-independent LARC preferred.

12Problem bleedingCoSRH

  • Common in the first 3 months — warn in advance, reassure.
  • Persists, new or post-coital: exclude pregnancy and STI (chlamydia), check adherence and interactions, cervical screening; examine the cervix.
  • Then: COC — try a different pill; POP, implant, DMPA — short course of a COC (if eligible) or mefenamic acid; or switch.

14Annual review checklistevery user, every year

  • BP and BMI (CHC users)
  • Smoking or vaping; age now 35+?
  • Migraine — new, or new aura?
  • New conditions or drugs → re-check UKMEC
  • Bleeding problems, side effects, satisfaction
  • STI risk, condoms, testing
  • Cervical screening up to date
  • LARC due dates; DMPA reassess at 2 years

16Aged 40+ and stoppingCoSRH over-40s

  • CHC: fine if UKMEC allows; stop at 50. DMPA: advise switching at 50.
  • POP, implant, LNG-IUD: continue to 55, then stop.
  • Non-hormonal: stop after 1 year without periods if 50+, 2 years if under 50.
  • HRT is not contraception.

17Refer or seek specialist adviceCoSRH · NICE CG30

LARC fitting if not trained in-house (start a bridging method today) · multiple UKMEC 3 or complex conditions · current breast cancer (Cu-IUD; oncology input) · non-palpable implant · lost threads with the device not seen on ultrasound (give EC and an interim method meanwhile) · under 16s needing extra support → sexual health service.

18Never-eventsCoSRH · UKMEC 2025 · MHRA

  • Never: CHC with migraine with aura · CHC without a recorded BP and BMI · UPA in a woman on an enzyme inducer, or a progestogen started within 5 days of UPA · an EC consultation without offering the Cu-IUD.
  • Never: stop or remove one method before the next is effective · tell her antibiotics stop the pill · valproate in a woman who could become pregnant without highly effective contraception · withhold contraception because a smear is overdue.
