Allergy test results CLINIC GUIDE

Adults and children with a positive specific IgE or skin prick test, raised total IgE or tryptase. Not for acute anaphylaxis care beyond first treatment, patch testing (dermatology) or immunotherapy decisions (specialist).

1Confirm it is realNG258 · CG116 · BSACI

Sensitisation ≠ allergyA positive sIgE or SPT only means IgE is present; read it against the history
Broad panelsGenerate false positives and needless exclusion diets: test only what the history suggests
Total IgENon-specific: atopy, parasites, rarely IgE myeloma
Tryptase timing (16+)Sample as soon as possible after treatment starts; second ideally 1–2 h, no later than 4 h from onset; baseline after recovery
Normal tryptaseDoes not exclude anaphylaxis, especially food or respiratory reactions
RangesAssay- and laboratory-dependent; SPT by trained staff with resuscitation facilities

4Work-up checklistCG116 · NG258 · CG183

  • Allergy-focused history
  • Onset: minutes vs hours
  • Exact trigger, cooked vs raw
  • Tolerated since?
  • Cofactors: exercise, alcohol, NSAIDs
  • Asthma control
  • Targeted sIgE only
  • FBC, eosinophils
  • C4 if angioedema alone
  • Drugs: beta-blocker, ACE inhibitor

No serum IgG, Vega, kinesiology or hair analysis (CG116). No serum IgE to diagnose drug allergy outside specialist care (CG183).

8After suspected anaphylaxis — the GP's jobNG258 · MHRA · BSACI

RecordTime of onset, features, treatment given, suspected trigger and cofactors.
TryptaseCheck timed samples were taken in hospital; baseline after recovery via the specialist pathway.
EquipTwo AAIs by brand, technique taught, written action plan, school or carer plan.
ReferSpecialist allergy service for every suspected anaphylaxis (NG258); venom → immunotherapy assessment.

9Urticaria and angioedemaBSACI · TA339 · SPC

  • Chronic spontaneous urticaria: wheals recurring over more than 6 weeks; allergy tests and routine bloods rarely help.
  • Regular second-generation antihistamine; if needed, off-label up-dosing to four times the usual dose. Omalizumab is specialist-initiated (TA339).
  • Angioedema without wheals: stop ACE inhibitors; check C4 and C1 inhibitor.
  • Wheals lasting over 24–48 h, bruising or pain → consider urticarial vasculitis.

10Everyday safetyFSA · Anaphylaxis UK

  • The 14 major allergens must be declared on pre-packed food; Natasha's Law covers food pre-packed for direct sale.
  • Ask staff about loose or freshly prepared food; carry an allergy card.
  • Anaphylaxis UK and Allergy UK: training, school and peer support.

11Annual review and monitoring checklistNG258 · MHRA · BSACI

  • Two in-date AAIs physically carried; expiry dates
  • Dose right for current weight (children)
  • Brand-specific technique shown; retrain if brand changes
  • Written action plan up to date; school plan for children
  • Asthma control: poor control raises the risk of fatal food anaphylaxis
  • New reactions, cofactors, new drugs (beta-blockers)
  • Referral made and specialist plan received
  • De-labelled allergy recorded as resolved; patient told in writing

12When and where to referNG258 · BSACI · CG116

999Anaphylaxis; tongue or laryngeal swelling
Urgent allergyAny anaphylaxis not yet referred · venom systemic reaction (immunotherapy assessment) · significant food reaction · raised baseline tryptase · angioedema without urticaria
Paediatric allergyFood anaphylaxis · severe eczema, multiple foods, poor growth · suspected eosinophilic oesophagitis
Specialist drug allergyHigh-risk drug reaction histories; clinically important labels
GP-managedRhinitis, sensitisation without reaction, uncomplicated pollen-food syndrome

13Pitfalls and never-eventsCG116 · CG183 · MHRA

  • Never prescribe an AAI or a food-exclusion diet for a positive test alone · never order broad IgE panels.
  • Never diagnose drug allergy with serum IgE in primary care · never de-label after a severe reaction.
  • Beta-blockers blunt adrenaline: tell the allergy team. Montelukast: neuropsychiatric warning (MHRA).
  • Never use serum IgG, Vega, kinesiology or hair tests · never use components alone to predict severity.

14Say it like thispositive test, no reaction

"The test shows your immune system recognises this food, but you've been eating it without any trouble, and that tells us more than the blood test does. You don't need to stop eating it."
