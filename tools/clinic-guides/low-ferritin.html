Low ferritin: iron deficiency CLINIC GUIDE

A low serum ferritin, with or without anaemia, in adults 18+. Not for pregnancy, children, dialysis or ESA care (renal team) or IBD under specialist care.

1Confirm it is realBSG 2021

  • A low ferritin is rarely false: ferritin is the single most useful test, and a value <15 µg/L is highly specific for iron deficiency.
  • Ferritin is an acute-phase reactant: a normal ferritin does not exclude iron deficiency when inflammation is present (also raised by infection, liver disease, malignancy). If deficiency is suspected, add CRP and TSAT; TSAT <20% supports deficiency.
  • Serum iron swings with time of day and recent meals or tablets: do not decide on it.
  • Check the Hb, MCV and MCH: iron deficiency can occur with a normal MCV, early or in mixed deficiency.
  • Repeat only if the result does not fit the picture; otherwise act on it.
  • Anaemia: Hb <130 g/L men, <120 g/L non-pregnant women; use local laboratory ranges.

5Work-up checklistBSG 2021 · NG20 · NG12

  • FBC with MCV, MCH
  • CRP; TSAT if inflamed
  • tTG IgA + total IgA
  • Urinalysis for blood
  • FIT in IDA
  • U&E/eGFR
  • B12, folate if mixed picture
  • HPLC if MCV low out of proportion

Coeliac serology only while eating gluten. Do not defer iron while awaiting tests unless colonoscopy is imminent.

7Who needs GI investigationBSG 2021 · NG12 · HTG690 · NG20

Every adult with IDAFIT; ≥10 µg Hb/g → colorectal suspected cancer pathway. Coeliac serology and urinalysis.
Men, post-menopausal womenGastroscopy + colonoscopy together (CT colonography if unsuitable) — even if FIT is negative. Young men too.
Pre-menopausal womenTreat iron and periods; investigate if aged 50+, red flags, strong family history or IDA out of proportion.
Negative endoscopyRecurrent or refractory IDA → small bowel (capsule endoscopy) and renal tract. Long-term iron if the cause is irreversible.

9Follow-up checklistBSG 2021

  • Hb at 2–4 weeks: expect a rise of ≥10 g/L in 2 weeks
  • No rise → adherence, ongoing loss, coeliac, mixed deficiency, wrong diagnosis
  • FIT, coeliac and endoscopy results reviewed and recorded
  • Iron for 3 months after Hb normalises, then stop
  • Ferritin when response is incomplete, it recurs or the cause is unclear
  • FBC periodically (about every 6 months at first) to catch recurrence
  • Declines investigation: informed decision recorded
  • Return the same day: black tarry stools, vomiting blood, chest pain, faint, worsening breathlessness

10Recurrence and the long termBSG 2021

  • Recurs after treatment: is the source still active (periods, NSAIDs, gluten)? Reinvestigate if not explained.
  • Cause irreversible or unknown after full investigation: long-term iron with a documented, reviewed plan.
  • H. pylori: test and treat if dyspepsia, or after non-explanatory endoscopy per the GI pathway.

12Special groupsBSG 2021 · NG24

  • Pregnancy: different Hb and ferritin thresholds — use the maternity pathway; IV iron only under obstetric care. IBD, coeliac, bariatric surgery: oral iron often poorly absorbed; IV iron via specialist or local pathway — previous surgery does not stop the search for other causes.
  • Frail or major comorbidity: weigh the risks and benefits of endoscopy with the patient and record the decision. Young men: investigate as for older adults. After transfusion: iron is still needed, and the cause still needs finding.

13Pitfalls and never-eventsBSG 2021 · NG12

  • Never put IDA down to diet in a man or post-menopausal woman.
  • Never treat a negative FIT as an all-clear in confirmed IDA.
  • Never arrange only one end: oesophagitis on gastroscopy does not exclude a colonic cancer.
  • A rising Hb on iron does not mean the cause is found.
  • Black stools on iron are expected; tarry, sticky stools are not.
  • IDA on anticoagulants or antiplatelets is still investigated.
  • No routine ferritin screening in well vegans or athletes; investigate deficiency as in anyone.

14Say it like thisplain English

"Your iron stores are low. The tablets will fill them back up, but they don't tell us why you ran low. Finding where the iron went is the important part, so please do the tests I'm arranging, even once you feel better."
