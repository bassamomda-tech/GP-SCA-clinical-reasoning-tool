Raised creatine kinase (CK) CLINIC GUIDE

A raised CK in adults 18+, including on a statin. Not for children, suspected acute coronary syndrome (use troponin) or known neuromuscular disease under specialist care.

Rhabdomyolysis = CK >10× ULN with renal impairment, or >50× ULN (NHS England pathway). Weakness, dark urine, AKI or hyperkalaemia move a patient up a tier whatever the number. CK peaks 1–3 days after injury, so an early value may still be rising.

3Focused history and examination — what discriminatesexamine strength, not just pain

Pain or weakness?Ache after exertion or on a statin is usually benign. True proximal weakness (rising from a chair, stairs, arms up) points to myopathy.
Drugs and substancesStatin or fibrate dates and dose changes, interacting drugs, antipsychotics, colchicine, hydroxy­chloroquine, retinoids; alcohol, cocaine, supplements.
PrecipitantsNew or eccentric exercise, trauma, seizure, surgery, long lie, heat, recent viral illness.
SignsHeliotrope rash, Gottron papules, wasting, calf hypertrophy, thyroid signs; dark urine; family history of muscle disease.

5Work-up checklistby history, not a routine package

  • Repeat CK at rest
  • U&E/eGFR, potassium
  • Urine dipstick
  • TSH
  • Calcium, phosphate, magnesium
  • Glucose, LFTs
  • ECG if potassium abnormal or unwell
  • Drug and supplement review

Dipstick positive for blood with few or no red cells supports myoglobinuria but does not prove it — act on the picture, do not wait for microscopy. Add vitamin D, inflammatory or autoimmune tests only when indicated.

7On a statin — the national CK bandsNHS England statin intolerance pathway · NG238

<4× ULN, tolerableStatin cause less likely. Look for other causes; continue, or reassess and restart at a lower dose or alternative statin.
4–10× ULN or intolerableStop the statin for 4–6 weeks. Rechallenge once symptom-free: lower dose or different statin.
10–50× ULNStop the statin. Check renal function; renal impairment → treat as rhabdomyolysis. Specialist advice if CK does not settle.
>50× ULNRhabdomyolysis: stop the statin, same-day hospital. Do not wait for urine myoglobin.

Before starting (NG238): persistent unexplained muscle pain → measure CK; >5× ULN → re-measure after 7 days; still >5× → do not start; raised but <5× → start at a lower dose. Do not measure CK routinely on a statin without symptoms. Several statins not tolerated → lipid specialist; consider non-statin options (NG238).

8Advice and the conversationplain English

  • Build up new or intense exercise gradually; drink enough; avoid training in extreme heat or when unwell
  • Some bodybuilding and weight-loss supplements contain stimulants — stop them while CK is raised
  • On a statin: in blinded trials most muscle aches were as common on placebo (Herrett et al., BMJ 2021) — a reason to rechallenge, not to give up

"Your muscle enzyme is raised, which is very common after exercise. Please rest from hard exercise for a week and we'll repeat it. If your urine goes dark like cola, you pass much less urine, or your muscles become very painful or weak, get seen the same day."

9Follow-up and monitoringNG238 · NHS England pathway

  • Recheck CK about 7 days after rest if well
  • Track the trend, not one value
  • Still raised → targeted secondary tests ± referral
  • Statin stopped: rechallenge once symptom-free
  • Weakness worsening off the statin → urgent specialist
  • After rhabdomyolysis: renal function recovered, culprit recorded
  • Hypothyroid: CK rechecked once euthyroid
  • CK recorded as a multiple of ULN with the recheck date
  • Return the same day: dark urine, passing much less urine, severe pain or weakness; 999 for collapse, confusion or a tense, swollen limb

Refer with serial CK as multiples of ULN with dates, statin dates, renal and urine results, and a clear statement on weakness.

11Pitfalls and never-eventsNG238 · MHRA

  • Never wait for CK or imaging in suspected compartment syndrome.
  • Never stop a statin for good on a mildly raised CK alone.
  • Do not check CK routinely on a statin without symptoms.
  • Do not use fixed absolute cut-offs: use multiples of ULN.
  • Pain alone is not weakness: examine proximal strength.
  • Check interactions before prescribing a macrolide with simvastatin.
  • CK also rises in heart muscle injury: chest pain needs troponin.
  • A high CK that does not fall after stopping a statin is not simple myalgia.
