1Is it real? Most mild rises are notSfE · local laboratory protocol

Stress of the sampleVenepuncture, exercise, sex, sleep, a breast examination or nipple stimulation all raise prolactin. Repeat rested; a cannulated sample if needle stress is suspected.
MacroprolactinBound, inactive prolactin reads as high. Ask for PEG precipitation when symptoms are absent or out of proportion; act on the monomeric result.
Pregnancy, drugs, thyroidPregnancy test, drug list, TSH before anything else. Dopamine blockers, SSRIs, opioids and oestrogens raise it.
Hook effectA large pituitary mass with only a modest prolactin → ask the lab to repeat on dilution. Check units (mIU/L vs µg/L) and sex-specific range.

AnyoneHeadache, visual change, features of low pituitary function; long-standing hypogonadism → bone loss

5Work-up checklistSfE · Endocrine Society

  • Repeat prolactin, rested
  • Macroprolactin (PEG)
  • TSH ± FT4
  • Pregnancy test
  • U&E/eGFR, LFTs
  • Full drug and supplement list
  • Visual fields: confrontation; formal if any doubt
  • LH, FSH, oestradiol or testosterone if hypogonadal
  • Ask: periods, libido, erections, discharge, headache
  • MRI via endocrinology, not first line

Galactorrhoea from prolactin is usually bilateral and milky; one-sided or bloody discharge is a breast question. Avoid repeated breast examination before sampling.

7Living with a prolactinoma — what to coverDVLA · CoSRH · cabergoline SPC

It is benignNot a cancer; most are controlled with tablets and never need surgery. The Pituitary Foundation has good patient information.
Taking cabergolineSame days each week, with food to ease nausea. Do not stop or change the dose without the specialist: prolactin can rise again within weeks.
DrivingA visual-field defect must be reported to the DVLA; no driving until fields are confirmed safe. Sleepiness on cabergoline → do not drive.
Contraception, bonesMicroprolactinoma: combined hormonal contraception is generally fine if otherwise eligible (CoSRH); macro → ask endocrinology. Weight-bearing exercise, calcium, vitamin D.

8Follow-up and monitoringSPC · MHRA · SfE 2019

  • Repeat after drug review: normal → stop investigating
  • Cabergoline: echocardiogram at baseline, then at 5 years on usual doses; sooner at higher doses or with new murmur
  • Ask about impulse-control problems (gambling, spending, hypersexuality, binge eating)
  • Sleepiness or sudden sleep onset → do not drive
  • Mood change, especially with past psychosis or mania
  • Pregnancy plans: agree with endocrinology before trying
  • Bone health if long hypogonadism or amenorrhoea
  • Dose changes and withdrawal: specialist decisions
  • Macroprolactinaemia: discharge, note it on the record

10Special groupsPituitary Society 2023 · ESE 2021 · NICE NG257

  • Pregnancy: plan with endocrinology. Microprolactinoma: the dopamine agonist is usually stopped once pregnancy is confirmed. Macroprolactinoma or tumour near the chiasm: specialist review throughout; new headache or visual change → same day.
  • Infertility: normalise prolactin first; ovulation usually returns. Measure prolactin in infertility only with an ovulatory disorder, galactorrhoea or a pituitary tumour (NG257).
  • Men and post-menopausal women: no menstrual clue, so tumours are found later and are more often macroadenomas — always check visual fields.
  • Antipsychotics long term: sustained rise → hypogonadism and bone loss; assess fracture risk and raise with the psychiatric team.

11Pitfalls and never-eventsSfE · SPC · MHRA

  • Never MRI a single unrested result before repeating it and checking TSH, pregnancy and macroprolactin.
  • Never stop an antipsychotic yourself; dopamine agonists can worsen psychosis or mania.
  • Never treat stalk compression with cabergoline, or trust a modest result beside a large mass (hook effect).
  • Never measure prolactin to monitor a pregnancy; it rises normally.
  • Do not send a raised prolactin down a cancer route.

12Reassure and say it like thisplain English

Most raised prolactin results are stress, drugs or macroprolactin. Prolactinomas are benign and usually controlled with tablets.

"Your prolactin reading is high, but most of it is a larger, inactive form that attaches to proteins in the blood and can't act on your body. It's a quirk of your blood test, not a disease, so you don't need a scan or treatment unless new symptoms appear."
