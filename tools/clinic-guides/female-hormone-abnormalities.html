1Is the result valid? Check before you interpretNG23 · PCOS 2023 · local laboratory

Pregnancy firstA pregnancy test before anything else in amenorrhoea. Then ask about breastfeeding and recent delivery.
Hormones on boardCombined contraception, high-dose progestogen and HRT make FSH, LH and oestradiol uninterpretable. Do not use FSH to diagnose menopause on them.
TimingProgesterone about 7 days before the expected period (day 21 only in a 28-day cycle). FSH varies: never diagnose POI on one result.
Assay and stressTestosterone: early-morning sample with SHBG, reliable assay. Prolactin rises with stress and breast stimulation; repeat rested, check macroprolactin.

4Work-up checklist — by phenotypeno universal panel

  • Pregnancy test
  • TSH if indicated
  • Prolactin, rested
  • FSH/LH ± oestradiol
  • Testosterone + SHBG
  • DHEAS, 17-OHP if indicated
  • Timed progesterone (fertility)
  • PCOS: BP, BMI, HbA1c, lipids
  • Pelvic USS when indicated
  • Medicine and contraception list

PCOS in adults: 2 of ovulatory dysfunction, hyperandrogenism, and polycystic ovaries on ultrasound or a raised AMH, after excluding other causes. Adolescents: both of the first two; no ultrasound or AMH. AMH is not used to diagnose POI.

5Usually reassuringNG23 · PCOS 2023

  • High FSH and LH after the menopause: expected, not a tumour marker.
  • A mildly raised prolactin after a stressful sample: often normal on a rested repeat.
  • Polycystic-looking ovaries with regular cycles and no hyperandrogenism: not PCOS.
  • A low progesterone taken on the wrong day: retime, do not label.

7When HRT is not the whole answerNG23 · MHRA

HRT unsuitableMenopause-specific CBT. SSRIs, SNRIs and clonidine are not first line for flushes alone. Fezolinetant: check liver function first (MHRA).
Vaginal symptomsVaginal oestrogen, alone or with HRT; continue as long as needed; no endometrial monitoring; report bleeding.
Low desireConsider testosterone if HRT alone has not helped (NG23); off-label, keep levels in the female range.
SupportPCOS: no single best diet; avoid weight stigma. FHA: eating-disorder services. POI: the Daisy Network.

8Follow-up and monitoringNG23 · PCOS 2023 · ESHRE

  • Possible POI: second FSH 4–6 weeks after the first
  • HRT or POI treatment: review at 3 months, then at least yearly — symptoms, bleeding, BP, contraception
  • POI: DXA at diagnosis; repeat interval by result and risk
  • PCOS: BP, BMI, lipids and glycaemic status at diagnosis; glucose or HbA1c every 1–3 years by risk; OGTT if high risk or planning pregnancy
  • PCOS: endometrial-protection plan agreed and coded
  • Mood screen in PCOS and POI
  • Unscheduled bleeding on HRT, or PMB → investigate
  • Fezolinetant: liver function before starting and as the SPC/MHRA advise

10Pitfalls — do not routinelyNG23 · PCOS 2023

  • Use the LH:FSH ratio for PCOS, or diagnose PCOS from "polycystic ovaries" alone.
  • Take progesterone on day 21 whatever the cycle length.
  • Check FSH or oestradiol to adjust HRT, or FSH on combined contraception.
  • Diagnose POI from one FSH, or use AMH to diagnose it.
  • Use CA-125 as a screening test, or scan the endometrium in asymptomatic PCOS.

11Special groups and never-eventsNG23 · MHRA · ESHRE 2024

  • Contraception: ovulation in PCOS is unpredictable, and spontaneous pregnancy occurs in POI — HRT is not a contraceptive. Antiandrogens (spironolactone, finasteride) are off-label for hirsutism: exclude pregnancy and ensure reliable contraception; finasteride is specialist-led.
  • On HRT: suspected VTE → stop and same-day assessment; specialist advice before any restart. Breast cancer history with vaginal symptoms → discuss with the breast or menopause team.
  • Never leave a woman with confirmed POI without a hormone-replacement discussion at each contact; non-treatment carries the greater risk to bone and heart.
  • Never attribute rapid virilisation to PCOS, or delay referral to repeat a testosterone when the clinical picture is convincing.

12Say it like thisplain English

"Your blood test shows your ovaries have slowed down earlier than usual. We'll repeat it in about six weeks to be sure. If it's confirmed, taking hormones until around the usual age of menopause protects your bones and heart — it replaces what your body should be making, rather than adding extra."
