Hypothyroidism in adults CLINIC GUIDE

For overt and subclinical primary hypothyroidism: the new result, the levothyroxine start and the annual review. Adults 18+. Not for pregnancy or preconception (specialist care, RCOG GTG76), children, central hypothyroidism or thyroid cancer.
NICE NG145 (2019) · NG12 · RCOG GTG76 (2025) · SPS · MHRA

Page 1 · Diagnose and start raised TSH → read the pattern → treat, trial or monitor

!Act todayNG145 · NG12 · RCOG GTG76

  • Myxoedema coma (missed doses, then drowsiness, confusion, hypothermia) → 999.
  • Low or normal TSH with a low FT4 = central hypothyroidism: never titrate on TSH. Features of adrenal crisis (hypotension, vomiting, low Na⁺, hypoglycaemia) → emergency hydrocortisone and admit. Stable → 9am cortisol and endocrinology before any levothyroxine.
  • Unexplained thyroid lump → consider a suspected cancer pathway referral (NG12), whatever the TSH. Stridor → 999.
  • Positive pregnancy test on levothyroxine → increase the dose straight away, urgent TFTs and joint obstetric–endocrine care (RCOG GTG76).

Avoid testing during acute illness unless thyroid disease is suspected.

2Confirm before you label itNG145

  • Subclinical needs a raised TSH on 2 occasions 3 months apart; many mild rises settle.
  • Consider TPO antibodies once with a raised TSH; do not repeat.
  • Transient causes: recovery from illness, recent thyroiditis (post-partum, subacute), amiodarone, lithium, immunotherapy, biotin assay interference.
  • Post-thyroid-cancer: the oncology team may want TSH suppressed — check the letter before "correcting" it.

4Work-up — every new diagnosisNG145 · selective

  • TSH + FT4
  • TPO antibodies (once)
  • Pregnancy status and plans
  • Drug and supplement review
  • Lipids, HbA1c (CVD risk)
  • ECG if bradycardia or cardiac disease

Selectively: FBC, ferritin, B12, folate if fatigue · coeliac serology if doses keep climbing · 9am cortisol if Addison's features · ultrasound only for a lump or goitre.

Do not order routine FT3, repeat antibodies or a scan for a normal neck.

Confirmed pictureAction

Trials show little symptom benefit from treating mild subclinical disease, especially in older adults.

7First doseNG145 · SPS

Under 65, no CVD1.6 micrograms/kg/day, rounded to the nearest 25
Examples60 kg → 100 micrograms; 80 kg → 125; 100 kg → 150
65+ or CVD25–50 micrograms/day, titrate up
Not T3Do not routinely offer liothyronine

Also start low in severe, long-standing disease. Angina on titration → drop back, go slower. Recheck TSH from 6 weeks after starting (box 11).

10Rising TSH? Before increasing the doseSPS · NG145

  • Adherence — daily? Only before blood tests?
  • Timing — empty stomach, same routine
  • New drugs — iron, calcium, PPI, oestrogen, enzyme inducers
  • Weight gain, new brand or product
  • Malabsorption — coeliac serology if doses keep climbing
  • Assay — biotin or discordant results → ask the lab

13Annual review checklisttreated and untreated

  • On levothyroxine: TSH once a year when stable
  • Symptoms both ways: tired and cold, or palpitations, tremor, weight loss
  • Taking it right? Interacting drugs or supplements?
  • Suppressed TSH → reduce the dose (AF and bone risk)
  • Women who could become pregnant: plans — dose rises early in pregnancy
  • Untreated subclinical: TSH + FT4 yearly if features of thyroid disease (e.g. previous thyroid surgery, raised antibodies); otherwise every 2–3 years
  • Lithium or amiodarone: TFTs 6-monthly (SPS)
  • CVD risk: BP, lipids once euthyroid, smoking

16Special groups and never-eventsRCOG GTG76 · NG145

  • Pregnancy: counsel women on levothyroxine to increase the dose as soon as a test is positive and to contact the practice; joint obstetric–endocrine care. NG145 does not cover pregnancy.
  • Older adults: a mildly raised TSH is common; treatment rarely helps symptoms and over-treatment harms.
  • Never: diagnose subclinical disease on one result · titrate central hypothyroidism on TSH · give levothyroxine before steroids in suspected Addison's · start T3 in primary care · let TSH stay suppressed without a cancer reason.
