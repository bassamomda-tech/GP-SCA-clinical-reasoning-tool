Page 1 · Assess and start screen → count and grade → one first-line combination for 12 weeks

!Act todayNICE NG198 · MHRA isotretinoin

  • Acne fulminans — sudden, severe, ulcerated or crusted inflammatory acne, often with fever, joint pain or feeling unwell → refer the same day to the on-call hospital dermatology team, to be seen within 24 hours.
  • Current suicidal thoughts or a plan → same-day crisis or mental health assessment. On isotretinoin: new low mood or self-harm → urgent contact with the prescribing team.
  • On isotretinoin: possible pregnancy → stop, pregnancy test, contact the prescriber the same day. Headache with visual disturbance → same-day assessment with fundoscopy (intracranial hypertension).

1Count, don't guessNG198 severity bands

BandAny one of
Mild to moderateAny number of comedones · up to 34 inflammatory lesions (papules, pustules) · up to 2 nodules
Moderate to severe35 or more inflammatory lesions · 3 or more nodules

The band decides which first-line options are open. It is not the same as isotretinoin eligibility.

Record scarring (ice-pick, rolling, boxcar; keloid on chest and back) and persistent pigmentation — both are reasons to consider referral.

2First visit checklistNG198

  • Lesion count, nodules, sites
  • Scarring, pigmentation
  • Mood, confidence, self-harm
  • Picking or squeezing
  • Drugs: steroids, anabolic steroids, lithium
  • Hirsutism, irregular periods (PCOS)
  • Pregnancy plans, contraception
  • Previous courses: what, how long

Not acne? No comedones + flushing → rosacea; papules round mouth after steroid → perioral dermatitis; recurrent nodules in axillae or groin → hidradenitis.

3Skin care — everyoneNG198 §1.2

  • Non-alkaline (pH-neutral or slightly acidic) skin cleanser twice daily.
  • Avoid oil-based and comedogenic products; remove make-up at night.
  • Don't pick or squeeze: it increases scarring.
  • Not enough evidence to recommend any particular diet.

"These creams work slowly — most people see a difference at around two months and the full effect at three. If they sting at first, use them every other night rather than stopping."

5Antibiotic rulesNG198 §1.5

  • No topical antibiotic alone. No oral antibiotic alone. Never a topical plus an oral antibiotic.
  • Oral tetracycline only with a topical non-antibiotic: adapalene + BPO, or azelaic acid.
  • Doses: lymecycline 408 mg or doxycycline 100 mg once daily.
  • Review at 12 weeks; beyond 6 months only in exceptional cases, reviewed every 3 months.
  • Write the stop date in the notes when you start.

6Pregnancy and planningNG198 · SPC

In pregnancy or when planning it: avoid topical retinoids and oral tetracyclines (tetracyclines also in breastfeeding). Use effective contraception or another option; BPO + clindamycin with caution (NG198 Table 1). Not tretinoin + clindamycin. Check each SPC.

9Judge at 12 weeksNG198 §1.5

At 12 weeksThen
ClearStop the oral antibiotic; consider maintenance
Improved, not clearContinue the oral antibiotic with the topical for up to 12 more weeks
Not improved, mild to moderateA different first-line option
Not improved, moderate to severe, no antibiotic yetAn option containing lymecycline or doxycycline
Not improved on an oral-antibiotic optionConsider referral (box 15)

10Before calling it a failureNG198 · SPC

  • Duration — a genuine 12 weeks?
  • Adherence — daily, on all affected skin, not spot-treating
  • Irritation — stopped for dryness? Alternate nights, moisturiser
  • Pairing — any antibiotic without BPO, retinoid or azelaic acid?
  • Aggravators — cosmetics, anabolic steroids, drugs, PCOS
  • Diagnosis — rosacea, perioral dermatitis, folliculitis?

12Isotretinoin — what the GP needs to knowMHRA DSU Oct 2023 · Oct 2025 · 22 Jan 2026 · PPP

  • Specialist only: started and monitored by a consultant dermatologist-led team. The GP never starts it.
  • Pregnancy Prevention Programme: highly teratogenic; contraception from at least 1 month before, throughout and for at least 1 month after; supervised pregnancy testing.
  • Oct 2025: follow-up may be remote after an in-person first appointment; supervised pregnancy tests may be done remotely; ask about sexual function at follow-up.
  • 22 Jan 2026: second prescriber no longer needed for under-18s; updated Acknowledgement of Risk Form for all patients; BAD audit of the measures to follow.
  • Mental health and sexual function assessed before and during treatment. Never with a tetracycline or vitamin A.

1312-week review checklistevery course

  • Lesion count and band now vs start
  • New scarring or persistent pigmentation
  • Mood and impact; self-harm asked about
  • Irritation, photosensitivity, bleaching
  • Oral antibiotic: stop date; total months so far
  • Contraception and pregnancy plans
  • Isotretinoin from dermatology? Recorded as a specialist drug
  • Courses completed — referral criteria met?

14Maintenance and relapseNG198 · maintenance

Not always needed; consider it for frequent relapse. First choice: adapalene + benzoyl peroxide. Not tolerated → adapalene, azelaic acid or benzoyl peroxide alone. Review after 12 weeks. Antibiotics are never maintenance.

Relapse after a good response → another 12-week course of the same or a different first-line option.

15Refer and never-eventsNG198 §1.4 · MHRA

  • Same day: acne fulminans (on-call dermatology, seen within 24 hours); suicidal intent.
  • Refer (consultant dermatologist-led team): acne conglobata, nodulocystic acne, diagnostic doubt. Consider referral: mild to moderate not responding to 2 completed courses; moderate to severe not responding to a course with an oral antibiotic; scarring; persistent pigmentary change; distress at any severity.
  • Mental health (consider): significant distress, self-harm, body dysmorphic disorder.
  • Co-cyprindiol (MHRA, SPC): assess VTE risk; never with another hormonal contraceptive; contraindicated with current or previous meningioma.
  • Never: antibiotic monotherapy · topical + oral antibiotic · tetracycline with isotretinoin · antibiotic as maintenance · retinoid or tetracycline in pregnancy.
