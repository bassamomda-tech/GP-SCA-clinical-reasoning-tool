Hypomagnesaemia: the low magnesium CLINIC GUIDE

Serum Mg below 0.7 mmol/L, or a low K⁺ or Ca²⁺ that will not correct, in adults 18+. Not for children, hospital IV treatment, obstetric magnesium sulfate or refeeding.

1Confirm it is realrepeat if unexpected or discordant

  • Serum Mg is about 1% of body magnesium: a normal level does not exclude depletion, and a low level often means a large deficit.
  • EDTA contamination (wrong tube order): falsely low Mg and Ca with a very high K⁺.
  • Haemolysis releases magnesium from red cells and can mask a low level.
  • Low albumin lowers total Mg; interpret with the albumin.
  • Repeat with U&E, adjusted Ca²⁺, phosphate if unexpected; do not delay action when the ECG or symptoms are concerning.

3Why magnesium matters to the other resultsSPS 2024

PotassiumLow Mg makes the kidney waste K⁺: potassium stays low until magnesium is replaced.
CalciumLow Mg blocks PTH release and action: calcium alone will not correct a low Ca²⁺.
The heartLong QT, torsades; digoxin toxicity at a "therapeutic" level. Check digoxin and Mg together.
PhosphateLow with refeeding and alcohol: check it with every low magnesium.

5Work-up checklistSPS 2024 · SPCs

  • U&E, eGFR: before any replacement
  • K⁺: often low too
  • Adjusted Ca²⁺, albumin
  • Phosphate
  • ECG: symptoms, low K⁺, QT drugs, heart disease
  • Glucose or HbA1c if relevant
  • Every medicine: PPI, diuretic, laxatives
  • Alcohol, diarrhoea, nutrition
  • Coeliac serology if malabsorption
  • Digoxin level if on digoxin and unwell

Chvostek's sign is non-specific; Trousseau's is more specific. Urine magnesium or fractional excretion helps separate renal from gut loss but is a specialist-level test for persistent, unexplained cases.

9Safety-net and self-care — say it, then document itSPS 2024

Call 999Palpitations with dizziness, fainting or collapse; a fit; painful muscle spasms; new weakness with trouble swallowing or breathing.
Practice todayCannot take or keep down the magnesium; worsening cramps, tremor or palpitations.
Taking itWith food, in divided doses. Diarrhoea → tell us; a lower dose often helps. Come for the repeat blood test.
DietNuts, seeds, wholegrains, pulses and green vegetables help, but do not replace treatment of a low level.

10Follow-up and monitoring checklistSPS 2024 · SPCs · MHRA

  • Repeat Mg with K⁺, Ca²⁺ and renal function after about 7 days of oral treatment (SPS)
  • Target: Mg within the laboratory range, with K⁺ and Ca²⁺ corrected
  • Stop: transient cause → 1–2 days after the level normalises (SPS); continue if the cause persists
  • Long-term supplement: Mg every 3–6 months (SPC), more often in CKD
  • Long-term PPI: consider Mg before and periodically, especially with digoxin or diuretics (MHRA)
  • Annual PPI review with a step-down trial (CG184)
  • Low despite tolerated oral Mg → adherence, diarrhoea, renal loss, all drugs
  • Cause and PPI indication recorded

12Special situationsSPS 2024 · MHRA · CG32 · SPCs

  • On digoxin: low magnesium (and low K⁺) increases digoxin toxicity at a usual level; check both if nausea, visual change, bradycardia or confusion.
  • QT-prolonging drugs (some antipsychotics, antimicrobials, methadone, anti-arrhythmics): correct magnesium and potassium first; ECG.
  • CKD: magnesium is excreted by the kidney and accumulates; seek renal advice before replacement in CKD 4–5, dialysis or transplant.
  • Alcohol dependence, eating disorders, very poor intake: think refeeding before restarting nutrition; same-day assessment if high risk (CG32).

13Pitfalls and never-eventsSPS 2024 · MHRA

  • Calling K⁺ or Ca²⁺ "refractory" before magnesium is checked and replaced.
  • Never start oral magnesium without an eGFR.
  • Never give IV or IM magnesium in general practice.
  • Trusting a "therapeutic" digoxin level when Mg is low.
  • Adding a QT-prolonging drug before low Mg is corrected.
  • Assuming a long-term PPI still has an indication.

14Say it like thisplain English

"Your magnesium is low. It's a mineral your muscles, nerves and heart need, and it also helps keep your potassium and calcium right. Your indigestion tablet may be stopping you absorbing it, so we'll look at that and give you a supplement. If it upsets your stomach, tell us and we'll lower the dose."
