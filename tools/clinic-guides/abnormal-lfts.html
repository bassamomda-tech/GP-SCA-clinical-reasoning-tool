Abnormal liver blood tests CLINIC GUIDE

Abnormal ALT, AST, ALP, GGT, bilirubin or albumin in adults 18+. Not for children, pregnancy or post-partum (maternity pathway), or liver disease already under specialist care.

The size of the ALT rise does not predict how serious the disease is (BSG 2017): bilirubin, INR, albumin, glucose, renal function and the patient decide urgency. Isolated raised INR: think warfarin, vitamin K deficiency or malabsorption first.

MuscleExercise, injury, myositis raise AST more than ALT: check CK

Fasting, illnessRaise unconjugated bilirubin in Gilbert's
Previous resultsCompare first: a long-standing stable result changes the pace

Do not simply repeat. Most abnormal results are still abnormal a month later. Repeat in 4–6 weeks only after a clear transient cause (stopped drug or alcohol, acute illness) — and send the aetiology screen alongside.

4Work-up checklist — the aetiology screenBSG 2017 · NG49

  • Liver ultrasound
  • HBsAg (hepatitis B)
  • HCV antibody, reflex RNA
  • Ferritin + TSAT together
  • AMA, ANA, SMA
  • Immunoglobulins
  • FBC: low platelets = portal hypertension
  • INR, albumin, U&E
  • HbA1c, lipids: metabolic risk
  • Caeruloplasmin if under 40 (Wilson's)

Screen whatever the level or duration of the abnormality (BSG). MASLD: FIB-4 <1.3 = low risk of advanced fibrosis (aged under 65); ≥1.3 → second-line test via the local pathway. NICE NG49: ELF ≥10.51 = advanced fibrosis → refer.

6MASLD — staging the fibrosisBSG 2017 · NICE NG49 · local pathway

1 · Confirm MASLDFatty liver on USS or metabolic risk, alcohol within lower-risk limits, negative aetiology screen.
2 · FIB-4 first(age × AST) ÷ (platelets × √ALT). <1.3 = low risk (under 65): lifestyle, recheck per local pathway.
3 · FIB-4 ≥1.3Second-line test: ELF or elastography via the local pathway. Do not mix thresholds from different pathways.
4 · ELF ≥10.51Advanced fibrosis → hepatology. ELF <10.51 → retest every 3 years (NG49).

A low FIB-4 makes advanced fibrosis unlikely but does not exclude it; FIB-4 is less reliable in younger adults, over 65 and during acute illness.

7Advice and the conversationNG49 · NG50

  • Alcohol: no more than 14 units/week, spread over 3 or more days; none at all with alcohol-related disease or cirrhosis
  • Gradual weight loss, 150 min/week of activity, stop smoking
  • Stop unnecessary supplements and herbal remedies
  • Hepatitis A and B vaccination if chronic liver disease or at risk (Green Book)

"Your liver test is a little off. On its own that doesn't tell us much, so rather than just repeating it I'm checking the common causes — fat in the liver, alcohol, medicines and viruses — and whether there is any scarring. Most people with a result like this can be looked after here."

8Follow-up and monitoringBSG 2017 · NG49 · NG50

  • 4–6 weeks after stopping a drug or alcohol, or after a transient illness
  • MASLD: weight, metabolic and alcohol review at 3–6 months
  • Low-risk MASLD: reassess fibrosis per local pathway (ELF every 3 years)
  • Cirrhosis: 6-monthly ultrasound ± AFP for liver cancer (NG50)
  • Statin: transaminases within 3 months of starting and at 12 months, then only if indicated
  • Hepatitis B: liver cancer surveillance as set by the specialist (CG165)
  • Discharge only when the cause is found or serious disease and advanced fibrosis are excluded
  • Return the same day: yellow eyes, fever with pain, swelling tummy, bruising, confusion

HBsAg positive: with the referral send HBeAg/anti-HBe, HBV DNA, anti-HCV and anti-HDV where appropriate, and offer an HIV test (CG165).

10Pitfalls and never-eventsBSG 2017 · NG238 · NG12

  • Never just repeat an unexplained result without the aetiology screen.
  • Never stop a statin for transaminases below 3× ULN.
  • Normal LFTs do not exclude cirrhosis, hepatitis B or C.
  • Do not label Gilbert's from one bilirubin or without excluding haemolysis.
  • Jaundice at 40+ is a suspected cancer pathway referral, not a wait-and-see.
  • Ask about herbal, over-the-counter and bodybuilding products.
  • Do not wait for an ALT threshold: the extent of the rise does not grade the disease.
  • Cirrhosis from any cause: advise complete abstinence from alcohol (NG50).
