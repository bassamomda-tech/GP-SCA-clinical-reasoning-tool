What this case tests

Show the learning points (spoiler: read after you have practised)
Behind most missed vaccinations are practical barriers and specific worries, not refusal. Explore without judgement, using open questions, and ask about past experiences of care. A fever after the 8-week vaccines is common (especially with MenB) and is not a contraindication; true contraindications are rare (confirmed anaphylaxis to a previous dose or component; live vaccines in significant immunosuppression or pregnancy). Catch-up principles: never restart a course; give missed doses as soon as possible; several vaccines can be given at the same visit. Since 1 January 2026, MMRV has replaced MMR in the routine schedule at 12 and 18 months for children born on or after 1 January 2025; UKHSA allows MMR to be offered where a parent prefers it. Leo needs his pre-school booster and second MMR-containing dose. Make access easy (combined, out-of-hours appointments) and document the parent’s decisions.
