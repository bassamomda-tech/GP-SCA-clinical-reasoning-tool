SCA dates 2026–27

The RCGP runs the SCA across nine diets a year. Each diet normally takes place over several exam days (the RCGP currently allocates four), and you are given a date within the diet. Results are published at 17:00 on the results day. The dates below are from the RCGP's exam dates and booking page and reasonable adjustments page. The RCGP notes that dates can change.

As a rule of thumb from the published dates, the final booking deadline falls about nine to ten weeks before the diet, and the reasonable adjustments deadline about four weeks before that.

The SCA fee

  • £1,207: the fee for the 2025–26 exam year. It still applies to candidates booked on diets from September to November 2026.
  • £1,238: the published fee for SCA diets sat from 1 January 2027 to the end of July 2027.

The RCGP reviews fees at the start of each academic year, so confirm the figure for your diet on the RCGP's important exam information page before you budget. Some employers, deaneries and study-budget schemes help with exam costs; ask your programme team what applies to you.

Booking and staged payments

  1. Book through MyRCGP. The RCGP's help pages describe booking in your MyRCGP account under My Exams → Book an upcoming exam. Your MRCGP (Associate in Training) membership must be active.
  2. Book early. You can book up to 12 months ahead, and cancelling or changing dates is free until the final booking deadline (RCGP: applying for the SCA).
  3. Pay in stages. Since 1 April 2026 SCA bookings use staged payments: a 25% deposit and a second 25% payment, with the balance due by the final booking deadline. Payments are not taken automatically, and a missed payment can mean your booking is cancelled. The exact timings for each instalment: check RCGP.
  4. Confirm eligibility by the final booking deadline. Full payment and eligibility must be in place by then, or provisional bookings are cancelled.

Who can sit the SCA

The RCGP says trainees are eligible to sit the SCA from ST3, and your training record must show you are in ST3. Several deanery pages describe this as being within 12 months of your expected completion of training (CCT) date. Under the MRCGP regulations, the AKT and SCA must be passed during training to apply for a CCT, with further attempts possible during deanery-authorised extensions. Attempt limits and rules for out-of-training candidates are in the MRCGP regulations check RCGP.

If you need reasonable adjustments (for example for dyslexia, a disability, a health condition, pregnancy or breastfeeding), apply by the deadline in the table. It is usually several weeks before booking closes.

Planning backwards from your diet

Pick the diet first, then plan revision backwards. Most candidates want 8–12 weeks of focused preparation (see the 12-week plan in the SCA Guide), with two full mock exams in the last six weeks. Frequently asked questions

When are the next SCA exam dates?
Published diets include 3–6 November 2026 (results 8 December 2026), 12–15 January 2027, 9–12 February 2027 and 9–12 March 2027. Always confirm on the RCGP exam dates page, as dates can change.
How much is the SCA fee?
The 2025–26 fee was £1,207, which still applies to diets from September to November 2026. The RCGP has published £1,238 for diets sat from 1 January 2027 to the end of July 2027. Check the RCGP website for your diet.
Can I pay for the SCA in instalments?
Yes. From 1 April 2026 the RCGP uses staged payments: a 25% deposit and a second 25% payment, with the balance by the final booking deadline. Payments are not taken automatically, so diarise each one.
How do I book the SCA?
Through your MyRCGP account (My Exams, then Book an upcoming exam), with active Associate in Training membership. You can book up to 12 months ahead, and changes are free until the final booking deadline.
When is the reasonable adjustments deadline?
It is set for each diet and falls weeks before booking closes: for example 9 November 2026 for February 2027 and 14 December 2026 for March 2027. Check the RCGP reasonable adjustments page for your diet.
Who is eligible for the SCA?
GP trainees from ST3 onwards, with ST3 shown on their training record. Deanery guidance describes this as within 12 months of the expected CCT date. Check the MRCGP regulations for attempt limits and out-of-training rules.
