How to get the most from five cases

  1. Do one cold, under exam timing. 3 minutes on the brief, 12 minutes out loud. Your first attempt shows your real habits.
  2. Mark it honestly against the three domains in Examiner Marking, then read the model consultation.
  3. Swap roles with a colleague. Playing the patient from the script is one of the fastest ways to learn what examiners notice. You hear what cue-missing sounds like from the other chair.
  4. Use your free AI run on a case you have not seen, so the feedback reflects how you consult rather than what you remember.
  5. Repeat the weakest case a week later. If the same domain slips again, that is your revision priority.
