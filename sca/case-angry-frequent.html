What this case tests

Show the learning points (spoiler: read after you have practised)
The angry frequent attender with normal results needs three things in strict order: the legitimate grievance conceded specifically (the sigh, the six minutes — relationship injury is the true presenting complaint), the exclusion stated with numbers and honest re-entry criteria (what WOULD reopen cardiac assessment), and a POSITIVE diagnosis with mechanism — panic’s hyperventilation physiology explains every symptom in order, which "all your tests are normal" never does. Referral is neither granted to end the call nor refused as a power move: it’s parked transparently behind a dated, same-doctor plan. The hidden agenda — a coffin carried at 44, a body standing guard ever since — is offered as physiology for HIM to connect, never as "just grief". Continuity is the actual prescription.
