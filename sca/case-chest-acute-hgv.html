What this case tests

Show the learning points (spoiler: read after you have practised)
Telephone chest-pain triage is diagnosis by ear: central heavy pain with autonomic features in a high-risk patient is ACS regardless of the word the patient chooses — and a crescendo of exertional episodes is the two-week warning. The clinical actions are scripted (NICE CG95: 999 from the call, location, a single 300 mg aspirin loading dose chewed, nobody drives); the consultation’s real work is the hidden agenda — here the Group 2 licence, answered with honest DVLA specifics (revoked after ACS, relicensing considered from at least 6 weeks if symptom-free with satisfactory functional testing and LVEF at least 40% — pause-and-reinstate, not career death) and the practical chain (dispatcher, load, lorry) solved so the patient can afford to say yes to the ambulance.
