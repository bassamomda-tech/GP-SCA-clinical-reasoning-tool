What this case tests

Show the learning points (spoiler: read after you have practised)
New severe weight-bearing pain in a metastatic femur is a pathological fracture until imaged — same-day assessment, handling advice, and the surgical/radiotherapy options named with hope. The opioid arithmetic is examinable: BNF breakthrough dose = usually 1/10 to 1/6 of the 24-hour background (here 6–10 mg; 5 mg is below the range), and regular need for several PRNs a day means the background needs review — with safety checks (sedation, myoclonus, renal function, bowels) before escalation. The hidden agenda is double: the morphine-kills myth (answer it: titrated opioids treat pain, they don’t shorten life) and the unopened DNACPR envelope — reframed from verdict to protection, opened together, never alone.
