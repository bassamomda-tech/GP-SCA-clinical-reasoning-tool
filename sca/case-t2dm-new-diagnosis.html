What this case tests

Show the learning points (spoiler: read after you have practised)
The new-T2DM consultation is not a data download — the HbA1c is the easy part. The examinable core here is the hidden agenda: fatalism built on a father lost to untreated complications, plus an unvoiced terror about the driving licence and livelihood. A prescription handed into that despair changes nothing. The skill is to treat the fatalism first — his father’s untreated course in another era is not Errol’s fixed fate; complications come from years of uncontrolled glucose/BP/lipids/smoking, all modifiable — and to answer the DVLA question proactively and accurately — having asked what he drives: on a car/van licence (Group 1), diet, metformin or an SGLT2 inhibitor means no notification and no licence loss; a Group 2 lorry licence would need notification on tablets. Then negotiate a plan built around his actual day, lead with smoking cessation as highest-yield CVD action, offer remission as a real goal given early onset (NHS Type 2 Diabetes Path to Remission Programme), start treatment per NICE NG28 (updated February 2026: metformin then an SGLT2 inhibitor), and leave him with one concrete step and genuine agency.
