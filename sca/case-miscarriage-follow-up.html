What this case tests

Show the learning points (spoiler: read after you have practised)
A telephone follow-up after early miscarriage has a safety part and a human part. Safety: screen for heavy bleeding, pain, fever and offensive discharge; after medical or expectant management NICE NG126 advises a urine pregnancy test at 3 weeks, with review if positive — a faint positive at 2 weeks is often normal. Anti-D is no longer recommended for miscarriage up to 11+6 weeks (NG126, 2026 update). The human part: treat it as a bereavement, address self-blame directly (most losses are chromosomal and not caused by anything she did), advise on the next pregnancy, discuss assessment after two losses, check mood and partner support, and offer practical help and follow-up.
