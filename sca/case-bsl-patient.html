What this case tests

Show the learning points (spoiler: read after you have practised)
Accessible communication is part of clinical safety. With a BSL interpreter on video: check consent and privacy, speak to the patient (not the interpreter), take turns, allow for the lag, and check understanding by teach-back. Adapt red-flag questions — for a signer, “difficulty speaking” becomes “difficulty signing” and hand weakness matters. Migraine with aura is UKMEC 4 for combined hormonal contraception: stop it and offer a same-day progestogen-only method or LARC. Under the Accessible Information Standard the practice must identify, record, flag, share, meet and review communication needs — interpreters booked automatically, text or email contact, longer appointments — and never rely on family members to interpret.
