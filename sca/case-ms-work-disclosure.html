What this case tests

Show the learning points (spoiler: read after you have practised)
Return-to-work consultations test whether the doctor can turn medical facts into practical, lawful advice. MS (like cancer and HIV) is deemed a disability under the Equality Act 2010 from diagnosis, so the employer must not discriminate and must consider reasonable adjustments — disclosure is voluntary but usually helps. The fit note (“may be fit for work”) is a powerful tool: be specific about phased return, amended duties, altered hours and workplace adaptations. Occupational Health and the Access to Work scheme add support. Always ask about driving in neurological conditions: anyone with MS must tell the DVLA, which then decides; the GP explains the duty and records the advice, and GMC guidance describes when a doctor may need to inform the DVLA if a patient continues to drive when unfit. Heat-related worsening (Uhthoff phenomenon) and fatigue are common and should shape adjustments. Offer the annual flu vaccine (infection can trigger relapse) and keep the MS nurse involved.
