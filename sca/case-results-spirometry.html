What this case tests

Show the learning points (spoiler: read after you have practised)
COPD is diagnosed by symptoms and risk factors plus post-bronchodilator spirometry showing FEV1/FVC below 0.7; severity is graded by FEV1 % predicted (mild 80% or more with symptoms, moderate 50–79%, severe 30–49%, very severe under 30%). Large reversibility (over 400 mL) suggests asthma. Explain spirometry in plain language. NICE management: smoking cessation is the most effective intervention (offer behavioural support plus pharmacotherapy — combination NRT or varenicline — and e-cigarettes as an option); offer pneumococcal and annual flu vaccination (and COVID-19 as eligible); pulmonary rehabilitation for those functionally limited (MRC 3 or more, or 2 if limited); a personalised self-management plan; optimise treatable traits and comorbidities. Inhalers: SABA or SAMA first; if still limited and no asthmatic or steroid-responsive features, LABA+LAMA; consider LABA+ICS only with asthmatic features or raised eosinophils. Check technique before escalating. Address emotional responses (fear from a family history, shame about smoking) to support engagement.
