What this case tests

Show the learning points (spoiler: read after you have practised)
NICE NG12: age 60+ with persistent unexplained back pain plus anaemia, raised ESR/plasma viscosity, hypercalcaemia or renal impairment = suspected myeloma — NG12 asks for FBC, calcium, ESR/plasma viscosity, serum protein electrophoresis and serum free light chains together (urine Bence-Jones only if free light chains are unavailable), then a suspected cancer pathway referral if the results suggest myeloma. Screen cord compression and hypercalcaemia the same call, and stop nephrotoxic NSAIDs immediately. The hidden agenda — a man who watched late diagnosis kill his wife and has been "getting things in order" instead of attending — is answered with honest asymmetry, not reassurance.
