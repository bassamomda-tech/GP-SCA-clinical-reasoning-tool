What this case tests

Show the learning points (spoiler: read after you have practised)
The marking trapdoor here is the patient asking for antibiotics — accepting the framing makes you miss the suspected cancer pathway referral. The single highest-yield behaviour is slowing down on the throwaway phrase ("a streak of blood") and naming the worry explicitly. NICE NG12: ≥40 y/o smoker with unexplained cough → urgent CXR.
