What this case tests

Show the learning points (spoiler: read after you have practised)
OA in primary care is non-operative and patient-centred. The marking gap is rarely the medicine — it's noticing the bereavement subtext, naming what the walk means, and negotiating rather than dictating. Imaging adds nothing when the diagnosis is clear and surgery is not the answer.
