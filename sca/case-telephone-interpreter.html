What this case tests

Show the learning points (spoiler: read after you have practised)
With a professional interpreter on a three-way telephone call, the doctor’s job is to make the interpreting easy: brief the interpreter, check the patient is comfortable and private, speak directly to the patient in short chunks, avoid jargon and check understanding with teach-back — a polite “yes, yes” is not understanding. Clinically, HbA1c 88 with osmotic symptoms is type 2 diabetes in a 46-year-old with central obesity, but ketones and red flags still need checking. NICE NG28 (2026) now recommends modified-release metformin plus an SGLT2 inhibitor first-line for most adults, so sick-day rules, ketone awareness and caution during prolonged religious fasting matter. Find the fear behind “is it serious?” and give honest, specific reassurance; then fix the communication route so results never again reach him through his child.
