What this case tests

Show the learning points (spoiler: read after you have practised)
Erectile dysfunction after MI is usually multifactorial: vascular disease, medicines (beta-blockers, thiazides), anxiety about exertion, and relationship strain. Always ask about medicines bought online or over the counter — unregulated “herbal” or generic products often contain undeclared or variable doses of PDE-5 inhibitors. PDE-5 inhibitors are absolutely contraindicated with nitrates (GTN, isosorbide) and nicorandil because of profound hypotension; sildenafil SPCs also list recent MI or stroke and hypotension below 90/50 as contraindications. After an uncomplicated MI, sexual activity is usually safe once the patient can manage moderate exertion without symptoms. NICE suggests continuing a beta-blocker for 12 months after MI in people without reduced LVEF and then discussing whether to stop — a natural point to review bisoprolol with cardiology. Respecting a patient’s preference for fewer medicines often means reviewing what he already takes before adding something new.
