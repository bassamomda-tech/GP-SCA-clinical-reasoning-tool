What this case tests

Show the learning points (spoiler: read after you have practised)
Chronic widespread pain with fatigue, unrefreshing sleep and cognitive symptoms, normal inflammatory markers, thyroid and CK, and no synovitis or neurological signs is fibromyalgia — classified by NICE as chronic primary pain. The skill is a positive, validating diagnosis after a targeted screen for alternatives (inflammatory arthritis, connective tissue disease, PMR over 50, myositis, hypothyroidism, neurological disease, malignancy features), not a diagnosis of exclusion delivered as “there’s nothing wrong”. Address the specific fear (here MS) by naming the features that would change the plan; more imaging finds incidental abnormalities and reinforces illness worry. NICE recommends supervised group exercise programmes, psychological therapies (CBT or ACT), acupuncture within a defined course, and considering an antidepressant (amitriptyline, citalopram, duloxetine, fluoxetine, paroxetine or sertraline) for pain, quality of life and sleep after discussing benefits and harms. Do not start opioids, gabapentinoids or other antiepileptics, NSAIDs, paracetamol, benzodiazepines, antipsychotics or ketamine for chronic primary pain; if she is already taking them, review and agree a shared, gradual reduction. Daily codeine with morning headaches suggests medication-overuse headache. Screen mood and suicidal thoughts; support work with a fit note and adjustments.
