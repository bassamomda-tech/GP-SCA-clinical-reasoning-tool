What this case tests

Show the learning points (spoiler: read after you have practised)
A structured medication review in frailty starts with the person, not the list: what matters to them, how they actually take their medicines, and what the falls look like. Here the falls are explained by symptomatic postural hypotension on three antihypertensives, a thiazide-associated low sodium, an anticholinergic burden of 7, a hypnotic (sometimes doubled) and metformin overtreatment in a frail woman with an eGFR of 41. The skill is to prioritise and stage the changes, agree them with the patient who has capacity, involve the relative without letting her decide, and combine deprescribing with falls prevention, blood tests and early review.
