What this case tests

Show the learning points (spoiler: read after you have practised)
Pre-fit counselling for intrauterine contraception covers the woman’s priorities, UKMEC eligibility, a comparison of devices, risks with numbers, STI and pregnancy risk, and the fitting itself. Both the copper IUD and the 52 mg LNG-IUS are UKMEC 1 from 4 weeks postpartum, including while breastfeeding. The copper IUD is hormone-free, effective immediately, lasts up to 10 years, and can make periods heavier and more painful; the LNG-IUS usually reduces bleeding (first-line for heavy menstrual bleeding), needs 7 days of additional cover unless fitted early in the cycle (or continue the current method), and the 52 mg device is licensed for contraception for up to 8 years. Risks: perforation about 1–2 per 1000 insertions, about six times higher when breastfeeding; expulsion about 1 in 20; pelvic infection is uncommon and mostly in the first 20 days. Ask about previous examinations and birth trauma: trauma-informed adjustments and the explicit right to stop make fitting safer and more acceptable.
