The exam fee

The biggest single cost is the exam itself. According to the RCGP's important exam information page, the SCA fee is £1,207 for candidates already booked on diets in September to November 2026, and £1,238 for diets sat from 1 January 2027 to the end of July 2027. The RCGP reviews fees at the start of each academic year and explains its approach in its update on SCA fees. Staged payments are available; see SCA dates and fees.

You also need active RCGP Associate in Training membership to book. Membership and exam fees are separate from any preparation costs.

What the study budget may cover

GP trainees in England have access to a study budget managed by NHS England, but the rules on exam preparation are set out nationally and applied locally. What follows comes from published NHS England and regional pages. It is a guide to what to ask, not a promise that anything will be reimbursed.

  • Exam fees are not funded through study leave. NHS England's national study leave overview says so directly. It adds that travel and subsistence can usually be claimed for sitting exams required by your current curriculum.
  • One preparation course per exam is common. The London and Kent, Surrey and Sussex GP trainee support article describes funding one SCA preparation course, with your educational supervisor's agreement, and says question banks can be funded but count as that single course. Further courses go through a discretionary process.
  • Online resources are treated differently by region. The Wessex (South East) study leave A–Z allows one set of question banks from a recognised provider in place of a course, but excludes other online resources. The Thames Valley study budget FAQs (2023) say question banks and standalone online courses are not covered. The South West FAQs exclude online resources and subscriptions.
  • Approval usually comes first. Most schemes expect you to apply before you spend, with your educational supervisor or programme team agreeing the activity.
  • Study leave time is separate from money. Some regions allow a few days of study leave for private exam preparation; the West Midlands GP study leave page is one example.
Check before you buy. An online subscription such as ours may be claimable in some regions, as an exam preparation resource or in place of a course, and not in others. Check with your deanery or programme team, in writing if you can, before you rely on reimbursement. Trainees in Scotland, Wales and Northern Ireland should check with their own training organisation, as the rules differ.

Frequently asked questions

Can I use my study budget to pay the SCA exam fee?
No. NHS England's national study leave guidance says exam fees are not funded through study leave, although travel and subsistence for sitting a curriculum exam can usually be claimed. Check your own region's rules.
Can I claim an SCA preparation course from my study budget?
Often, one preparation course per exam may be claimable with your educational supervisor's agreement, but the rules vary by region. Check with your deanery or programme team before you book or pay.
Can I claim an online SCA subscription?
It depends on your region. Some allow a question bank or online resource in place of a course; others exclude online resources and subscriptions. It may be claimable, but check with your deanery or programme team first and do not rely on reimbursement.
How much does Reasoning GP cost for the SCA?
The Gold plan, which holds the SCA suite, is £15 a month, £40 every 3 months or £100 a year. Platinum, which adds the clinic toolkit, is £20 a month or £180 a year. See the pricing page for current prices.
Can my training programme pay for access?
Yes. Group access for practices, VTS schemes and training programmes can be invoiced. Use the group enquiry on the pricing page.
Can I cancel after my SCA?
Yes. Cancel at any time from Manage my plan. No further payments are taken and access continues to the end of the period you have paid for. There is no 14-day cooling-off refund, because access starts immediately.
