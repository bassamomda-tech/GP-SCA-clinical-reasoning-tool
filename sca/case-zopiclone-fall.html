What this case tests

Show the learning points (spoiler: read after you have practised)
Z-drugs and benzodiazepines raise the risk of falls and fractures in older people, and tolerance makes long-term use ineffective for sleep. The exam skill is deprescribing as a negotiation: validate the fear, harness the patient’s own motivation, taper slowly (never stop abruptly), and replace the tablet with something — CBT-I principles and attention to the loneliness driving the 3am waking. Escalating to temazepam is the station’s trapdoor.
