What this case tests

Show the learning points (spoiler: read after you have practised)
An unwell care-home resident who is “not herself” needs observations, not a dipstick. Acute confusion with NEWS2 of 7 or more is an emergency whatever the presumed source — here likely a chest infection with sepsis. In people over 65, urine dipsticks are not used to diagnose UTI because asymptomatic bacteriuria is common; treating the dipstick both misses the real diagnosis and drives antibiotic resistance. The ReSPECT form guides the level of treatment: not for CPR does not mean not for hospital. On the telephone, a clear, ordered plan with practical steps and support for the carer is what keeps the resident safe.
