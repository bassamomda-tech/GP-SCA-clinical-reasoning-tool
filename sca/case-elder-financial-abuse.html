What this case tests

Show the learning points (spoiler: read after you have practised)
Financial abuse of older people often presents as a health problem — weight loss, cold, missed medicines, missed appointments — with a relative who answers for the patient. See the person alone (on video, check the room), ask open questions about who manages money and shopping, and ask directly about safety. Under the Care Act 2014 an adult with care and support needs who is experiencing, or at risk of, abuse or neglect and cannot protect himself because of those needs should be considered for a safeguarding enquiry. A person with capacity has the right to make decisions about his own life; seek consent for a referral, explain what will happen, and know the exceptions (risk to others, serious crime, coercion or undue influence, vital interests). Treat the health effects too, and follow up soon.
