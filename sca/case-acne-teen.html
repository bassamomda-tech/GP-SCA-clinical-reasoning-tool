What this case tests

Show the learning points (spoiler: read after you have practised)
Adolescent acne is almost always also a psychological consultation. The marking failure is letting a parent dominate the airtime and leaving without hearing from the patient themselves. Topical adapalene/BPO is the first-line structure NICE recommends; oral antibiotics are time-limited adjuncts; isotretinoin is consultant-prescribed.
