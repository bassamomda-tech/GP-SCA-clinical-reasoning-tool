What this case tests

Show the learning points (spoiler: read after you have practised)
A child with thirst, polyuria, new bedwetting, weight loss or tiredness has type 1 diabetes until proven otherwise. If capillary glucose is high (over 11 mmol/L), or even on symptoms alone, refer the same day to the paediatric diabetes team — phone them. Do not arrange fasting glucose, HbA1c or a glucose tolerance test first: they delay diagnosis and a quarter or more of children present in DKA. Screen for DKA (vomiting, abdominal pain, Kussmaul breathing, drowsiness, ketotic breath): if present, this is an emergency (999). Explain clearly that type 1 diabetes is autoimmune and not caused by diet, and help the family overcome practical barriers to attending today. Video lets you see the child — use it.
