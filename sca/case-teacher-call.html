What this case tests

Show the learning points (spoiler: read after you have practised)
A third-party safeguarding call runs on three rails at once: information flows IN freely (and the GP’s own records — the implausible A&E story, the DNAs, the HV note — corroborate); confidentiality flows OUT only as safeguarding lawfully requires; and duties never transfer — the DSL refers, the practice refers in parallel, neither waits. The hidden agenda is the caller’s: social entanglement and an "informal" school culture have paralysed her for two weeks, and the consultation’s real work is converting her own sentence into her own referral, today, without shame.
