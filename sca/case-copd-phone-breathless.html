What this case tests

Show the learning points (spoiler: read after you have practised)
Telephone triage of "more breathless" in a known COPD patient must NOT anchor on "just another flare". A structured history actively distinguishes a typical infective exacerbation (gradual build, increased purulent sputum) from SOMETHING NEW: PULMONARY EMBOLISM (sudden onset, pleuritic chest pain, haemoptysis, unilateral calf swelling, immobility/long travel/recent surgery/prior VTE, with tachycardia and breathlessness disproportionate to a clear chest found only on examination), HEART FAILURE (orthopnoea, paroxysmal nocturnal dyspnoea, ankle oedema, weight gain), PNEUMONIA (fever, focal symptoms, confusion in the elderly), PNEUMOTHORAX (sudden unilateral breathlessness/pain), arrhythmia, and lung cancer (haemoptysis, weight loss — NICE NG12 (Suspected cancer: recognition and referral, last updated April 2026)). Telephone assessment can take a careful history and gauge severity (speech in sentences, exhaustion, confusion) but cannot examine, take a pulse, measure saturations or perform an ECG, and with PE likely (Wells more than 4) NICE NG158 moves straight to an immediate CTPA or interim anticoagulation rather than a D-dimer, so any PE picture, concerning "not a flare" feature, or significant severity mandates URGENT face-to-face assessment or admission rather than a phoned-in rescue pack; only a clear-cut mild flare with a negative red-flag screen is suitable for remote management (prednisolone per NICE NG115; an antibiotic only on NICE NG114 judgement). The hidden agenda — exhaustion, inconvenience and minimisation ("just call it in, I’m too wiped out to come") — must not deflect the discriminating questions; when red flags emerge (here sudden onset, pleuritic pain, a swollen calf and a recent long coach journey — a PE picture), explain clearly and kindly why she must be seen urgently, arrange the route to assessment, refuse to collude with the phone-prescription request, and safety-net robustly with explicit 999 red flags.
