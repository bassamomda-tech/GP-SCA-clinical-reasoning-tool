What this case tests

Show the learning points (spoiler: read after you have practised)
Results that strongly suggest cancer must be acted on the same day, whatever the logistics: a suspected cancer pathway referral can be made now and redirected later; waiting for a patient to “settle” is a serious error. For an adnexal mass, NICE recommends calculating the Risk of Malignancy Index (U × M × CA-125); an RMI of 250 or more warrants specialist gynaecological oncology MDT care, and an ultrasound suggestive of ovarian cancer meets the NG12 criteria for a suspected cancer pathway referral. Breaking bad news by video needs the same structure as face to face (SPIKES: setting, perception, invitation, knowledge, emotions, strategy) plus checks on privacy and connection. People without a fixed address — boaters, Gypsies and Travellers, people who are homeless — are entitled to register with any GP practice without proof of address. Protect continuity with multiple contact routes, written copies of results, a named contact and a booked follow-up call.
