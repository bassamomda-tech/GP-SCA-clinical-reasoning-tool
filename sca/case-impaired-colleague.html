What this case tests

Show the learning points (spoiler: read after you have practised)
A concern that a colleague is impaired is a patient-safety issue first and a welfare issue at the same time. GMC Good medical practice (2024) requires doctors to act promptly when patient safety may be compromised — it overrides personal and professional loyalty — and to support colleagues. Deal with today first (a private, supportive conversation before the next clinical session; stand him down if impaired; get him home safely), recognise that doctors with alcohol problems and life crises are at increased risk of suicide, then escalate proportionately within the practice and to the responsible officer, while signposting confidential help (NHS Practitioner Health, LMC, occupational health). Never promise confidentiality you cannot keep, document facts, and protect the person who raised the concern.
