# Owner decisions, 19 Oct 2026 (facts register pass 1): ALL ACCEPTED

1. **Salbutamol in under-5s, acute wheeze:** use BTS/SIGN 158 wording everywhere: 2–10 puffs via spacer, titrated to response; 10 puffs if severe. Remove the "local protocol" fixed 4-puff regimen.
2. **Methotrexate before conception (UC case and any IBD page):** "Follow the specialist team. BSR advises stopping 1 month before conception for women and no routine stop for men on ≤25 mg/week; the SPC and some IBD guidance are more cautious."
3. **Insulin sick-day rules:** remove the "total daily dose >54 units" cut-off and the "doubling rule above 50 units". Use: "contact the diabetes team early if on high insulin doses or if glucose/ketones are not settling". Apply to the page, its print twin and its summary.
4. **Paracetamol in heavy drinkers (site-wide):** "Reduce the dose: commonly maximum 3 g/day with risk factors; some SPCs advise 2 g/day in chronic heavy alcohol use — check the SPC."
5. **Metformin and B12 (site-wide):** remove the ">4 years" rule and its MHRA attribution. Use the MHRA wording: "check B12 if symptoms of deficiency; consider periodic monitoring if risk factors (e.g. higher dose, longer duration, low-normal baseline)".
6. **CKD referral:**
   - Use NG203 criteria: 5-year KFRE risk >5%, ACR ≥70 mg/mmol (unless known diabetes and appropriately treated), sustained fall in eGFR of ≥25% with a change in category or ≥15 mL/min/1.73m² within 12 months, poorly controlled hypertension on 4+ drugs, known or suspected rare/genetic cause, suspected renal artery stenosis.
   - Keep "eGFR <30 not known to nephrology" but label it "commonly used locally", not NG203.
7. **Hypothyroidism in pregnancy (RCOG GTG76 2025):**
   - "TSH within the trimester-specific reference range, aiming below 2.5 mU/L pre-conception."
   - "Increase the levothyroxine dose by 25–30% (e.g. two extra doses a week) as soon as pregnancy is confirmed."
   - Align all pages; also add to the Medwise list.
8. **Frail HbA1c (dementia case and similar):** "an individualised, relaxed target agreed with the patient and carer (NG28); avoid hypoglycaemia". Remove "target 58 / 64" as targets.
9. **Gliptin stop rule:** "review at 6 months and stop if no meaningful HbA1c response". Remove the 5.5 mmol/mol (CG87) figure.

10. **H. pylori bismuth quadruple:** keep metronidazole 400 mg BD for 7 days. Wording:
    > "Bismuth quadruple therapy (penicillin allergy with previous clarithromycin exposure): PPI twice daily + bismuth + tetracycline 500 mg four times daily + metronidazole 400 mg twice daily for 7 days (NICE)."

    Add the caveat:
    > "If using Pylera®, follow the product-specific regimen: three capsules four times daily for 10 days, with a PPI twice daily. Do not substitute the Pylera schedule for the conventional component regimen."

    Optional note where space allows:
    > "Some areas reserve bismuth-containing regimens because of supply issues — check local guidance."

    Do NOT name any region. Maastricht VI (10–14 days) is European consensus; do not adopt it.
