- **Hypothyroidism in pregnancy (RCOG GTG76 2025):**
  - Before conception, aim for TSH ≤2.5 mU/L in overt hypothyroidism and severe subclinical hypothyroidism. Consider the same target in subclinical hypothyroidism, particularly if TPO-antibody positive.
  - When pregnancy is confirmed, increase levothyroxine by about 25–30% immediately: either double the dose on 2 days each week, or increase by 25 micrograms/day if the pre-pregnancy dose is ≤100 micrograms/day, or 50 micrograms/day if >100 micrograms/day. Arrange thyroid function tests.
