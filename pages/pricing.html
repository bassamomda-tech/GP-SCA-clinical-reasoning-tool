QuestionsThe things people ask before paying

Do I need a card to start?

No. The free pages need no card and no account at all. You only sign up when you want a paid plan, or when you have a code to redeem.

Can I cancel whenever I want?

Yes. Subscriptions are managed in your PayPal account and you can cancel there at any time. Access continues to the end of the period you have already paid for.

What happens to my account if I stop paying?

It reverts to the free plan — you keep the free pathways, protocols, cases, Scribe and the SCA Guide, and anything saved in your browser stays where it is. Nothing is deleted.

Is the clinical content actually reviewed?

Every clinical page carries a strip under its title stating when it was last reviewed, when it is next due and which guidance it is anchored to. The whole library was signed off on 20 September 2026, and high-risk pages are re-reviewed every six months. See how we keep content safe.

Can my practice pay by invoice?

Yes — use the group enquiry above. Individual plans go through PayPal; group access can be invoiced.

Is there a student or trainee discount?

Gold has a £40 three-month option built for the run-up to the exam, which is the cheapest way to cover an exam attempt. For a whole cohort, ask about group access.

What about the AI features and my patients' data?

AI features are for education and de-identified drafting only, and you should never enter patient-identifiable data. What each tool is and is not for is set out in AI safety and data.
