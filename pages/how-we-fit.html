Using us alongside national guidance

How we fit with NICE, the BNF and your ICB

NICE guidance and the BNF are free to use in the UK, thorough, and the authority. Reasoning GP does not replace them. This page says plainly what each one is for, what we add, how to use them together, and which source wins when they differ.

The authoritiesWhat NICE, the BNF and your ICB are for

NICE guidance

NICE guidelines set out the recommendations for diagnosing and managing conditions in the NHS, with the evidence behind each one. Go there for the full detail and the reasoning behind a recommendation.

The BNF and BNFC

The British National Formulary (adults) and BNF for Children: the reference for doses, interactions, cautions, contraindications, side-effects, and use in renal impairment, pregnancy and breastfeeding. Doses are always the BNF’s.

Your ICB formulary and local guidelines

Your integrated care board and area prescribing committee decide the local choices: which drug or device is first choice, antimicrobial choices, shared-care arrangements and referral routes. These routinely differ from the national default.

These remain the authority, together with NICE guidelines and MHRA safety advice. Where anything on Reasoning GP differs from them, they win — see If guidance differs.

If guidance differsWhich source wins

If guidance differs, follow this order

  1. MHRA safety alerts and Drug Safety Updates take precedence on safety restrictions, contraindications and warnings, from the date of issue.
  2. Then NICE guidance.
  3. Then the BNF or BNFC — and the BNF is always the source for doses.
  4. Then local guidance — your ICB formulary and local pathways. Where national guidance leaves the choice open (which drug, which device, which referral route), your local pathway is the one that applies.

Reasoning GP sits below all of these. If a page here disagrees with them, follow them — and tell us, so we can correct it. Our full evidence standard is on How we keep content safe.

BoundariesWhat we are not

  • Not a substitute for clinical judgement, your local protocol, or emergency assessment. In an emergency, call 999.
  • Reference and education, not a medical device. Reasoning GP publishes educational and reference content. It does not make recommendations about an individual patient, and we make no regulatory claim of any kind. See the intended purpose.
  • No patient data. Nothing on these pages needs patient details. Do not enter anything that identifies a patient into any tool on this site — Reasoning GP is not commissioned by your practice or ICB and is not an approved clinical system. See AI safety and data.
  • Not a prescribing authority. Check every dose in the current BNF or BNFC before prescribing.
  • Independent. Reasoning GP is not affiliated with, licensed by, endorsed by or produced in partnership with NICE, the BNF or any other guideline publisher. Linking to NICE and the BNF is for your convenience and does not imply that their publishers have reviewed or approved this site. Our pages are written from the primary published sources named on each page, not copied from any summary service.
