How this page works

  • Where the dates come from. Protocols, algorithms and cases: the review strip under each page title (assets/provenance.js): the later of the page's own sign-off and the site-wide review round, then 6 months for high-risk topics and 12 months for everything else. Snapshots: their own printed “Reviewed … · next review …” line.
  • Status. Overdue: the next review date has passed. Due ≤ 3 months: it falls within the next three months. No date: the page shows no review date.
  • Guidance cited comes from governance/guidance-register.json: status and month last checked in the 6-monthly round. Snapshots the register does not list yet show their parent page's guidance.
  • Keeping it current. The list is a snapshot built by governance/build-review-index.mjs. After content changes, ask Claude to “rebuild the review index” and upload assets/review-index.json.
  • This page is not in any menu, the sitemap or the site search, and search engines are told not to index it.
